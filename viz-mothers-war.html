The Mother's War

Frances Hubbell sent four sons to the Civil War and waited at home in Champlain, New York, learning what befell them only when their letters arrived — days or weeks after the fact. This visualization maps the gap between events and the news that reached her.
"Two or three letters a day have come from the boys but nothing from Henry." — Mother Hubbell, October 2, 1862
