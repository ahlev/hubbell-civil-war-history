**HUBBELL LETTERS CONFIRM:** Jan-Apr 1864 Nashville/Stevenson AL; May-Aug 1864 Sherman's March through GA (Altoona, Buzzard's Roost, Kingston, Dallas, Marietta, Chattahoochee, Atlanta); Sep-Oct 1864 Hospital at Chattanooga; Dec 1864 Nashville

**GAP FILLED:**
- Between Ringgold (Nov 27, 1863) and Atlanta Campaign (May 1864): Duty at Bridgeport/Stevenson AL; veteran furlough Jan 1864
- Alexander was hospitalized at Chattanooga Sep-Oct 1864, so he missed the Occupation of Atlanta (Sep 2-Nov 15) and likely the March to the Sea
- Alexander's "Dec 1864 Nashville" letter: He may have been at a convalescent/hospital facility while his regiment was on the March to the Sea and at Savannah. This is a CRITICAL finding -- if Alexander was in Nashville in December 1864, he was separated from his regiment which was in Georgia/Savannah.

**IMPORTANT NOTE ON ALEXANDER'S HOSPITAL PERIOD:**
Alexander's letters place him at Hospital in Chattanooga (Sep-Oct 1864) and Nashville (Dec 1864). The regiment was at Atlanta (Sep-Nov), then on the March to the Sea (Nov 15-Dec 10), then at Savannah (Dec 10-21). Alexander appears to have been separated from the regiment from approximately September 1864 through at least December 1864, likely due to illness or wounds sustained during the Atlanta Campaign.

## GAPS IN HUBBELL LETTERS vs. REGIMENT MOVEMENTS

### Letters Alexander SHOULD have written about (but may not have):

1. **Aug 24, 1862 - Sulphur Springs**: Between Harpers Ferry letters and later ones
2. **Aug 29-30, 1862 - Groveton / Second Bull Run**: Major battles
3. **Sep 16-17, 1862 - Antietam**: The bloodiest single day of the war
4. **Nov 9, 1862 - Reconnaissance to Rippon**: Small operation
5. **Dec 2-6, 1862 - Expedition to Winchester**: Gap between Harpers Ferry and Fairfax
6. **Jan 20-24, 1863 - Mud March**: Between Fairfax and Aquia/Stafford
7. **May 1-5, 1863 - Chancellorsville**: Major battle
8. **Nov 23-25, 1863 - Lookout Mountain / Missionary Ridge**: He was there per letters
9. **Nov 27, 1863 - Ringgold Gap**: Immediately after Missionary Ridge
10. **Nov 15-Dec 21, 1864 - March to the Sea / Savannah**: Alexander was hospitalized, likely missed this
11. **Jan-Apr 1865 - Carolinas Campaign / Bentonville**: Unknown if Alexander participated
12. **May 24, 1865 - Grand Review**: Unknown if Alexander participated

### Key question for the Hubbell letter collection:
Alexander's hospitalization (Sep-Dec 1864) means he likely missed Sherman's March to the Sea entirely. Did he rejoin for the Carolinas Campaign? His next confirmed letter is Jun-Jul 1865 near DC. There is a ~6 month gap (Dec 1864 - Jun 1865) where his status is uncertain.
