# Discrepancy: James's death date — gravestone "Oct. 12, 1865" vs. family intro "Oct. 19, 1865"

- **Date filed:** 2026-06-06
- **Severity:** notable (vital date)
- **Status:** resolved (toward Oct 12, 1865)
- **Persons involved:** `PER-hubbell-james`
- **External sources involved:** Glenwood Cemetery four-brothers memorial, Champlain NY (NNY Tombstone Transcription Project; internal G11); NY AG 153rd roster; DOC-INTRO-1996

## What the family record says
The 1996 family introduction (DOC-INTRO) states: *"James Hubbell — Co. I, 153rd Reg't... James died on the way home from Savannah, Ga. **Oct. 19, 1865**."* The project README repeats Oct 19, 1865.

## What the record shows
The contemporaneous **gravestone** — the four-brothers Civil War memorial at Glenwood Cemetery, Champlain, erected by the Sabbath School of the First Presbyterian Church — reads, verbatim:
> James HUBBELL / of Co. I. 153 Reg. N.Y.V. / **Died at Albany Oct. 12, 1865**, while in the Service of his country / AE. 27 Yrs.

The NY AG roster independently shows James "mustered out with company, October 2, 1865, at Savannah, Ga." — so he survived to muster-out and died ~10 days later at Albany while traveling home.

## What's likely true
**James died October 12, 1865, at Albany, NY.** The gravestone is a contemporaneous, carved record corroborated by an independent transcription. The family's "Oct. 19" is uncorroborated and is **exactly the date of James's Cedar Creek wounding one year earlier (Oct 19, 1864)** — a near-certain conflation in family memory of the two October-19 milestones (wound 1864; death misremembered to match).

**Confidence:** `external · clear · definite` for Oct 12, 1865. A death record (NARA "Registers of Deaths of Volunteers, 1861-1865," or an Albany hospital register) would make it documentary beyond the stone; that is a handoff.

## Why this matters
1. A clean case where a **contemporaneous gravestone overrides a late family compilation**, and where the *error itself is explicable* (the Oct-19 anniversary), which strengthens the resolution.
2. It refines the family's signature line about James ("died on the way home") with a place (Albany) and a corrected date.
