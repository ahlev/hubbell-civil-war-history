# Discrepancy: Henry's birth year — genealogy "Nov. 1839" vs. roster/gravestone (age 23/24 → ~1837)

- **Date filed:** 2026-06-30
- **Severity:** notable (vital date)
- **Status:** resolved (toward ~November 1837)
- **Persons involved:** `PER-hubbell-henry`
- **External sources involved:** NY AG 34th Infantry roster (Phisterer; internal dossier transcription); Glenwood Cemetery four-brothers memorial, Champlain (G11); family genealogy registers (G07, G08); Chapin, *A Brief History of the Thirty-Fourth Regiment N.Y.S.V.* (1903)

## What the family record says
The genealogy registers **G07** and **G08** both give Henry **"b. Nov. 1839"** — which the bio followed ("age 21 at enlistment, age 22 at death").

## What the record shows
Two independent records of Henry's actual **age** converge on a birth ~1837–38, not 1839:
- **NY AG roster:** "Age, **23** years. Enlisted May 22, 1861" → b. ~Nov 1837–May 1838.
- **Glenwood gravestone (G11):** "Henry HUBBELL of Co. D 34 Reg. N.Y.V. fell at the battle of Antietam… **AE. 24 Yrs.**" → b. ~Sept 1837–Sept 1838.

## What's likely true
**Henry was born ~November 1837** (age 23 at the May 1861 enlistment, 24 at his Sept 1862 death). Only a Nov-1837 birth satisfies BOTH primary age records while preserving the family-recorded month ("November"). 

**Independent internal cross-check:** the genealogy is internally impossible at "Nov 1839" — it places brother **Charles at April 1840**, only ~5 months later (impossible for full siblings). Pushing Henry to Nov 1837 restores a clean ~2.5-yr gap (Fannie Nov 1835 → Henry Nov 1837 → Charles Apr 1840 → James Jan 1842/43 → Alexander/Julia twins Mar 1844). The genealogy's **year** is the error, not the roster/stone.

**Confidence:** `external · clear · definite` for "age 23 at enlistment / b. 1837–38"; `medium-high` for the precise "November 1837" (month from family tradition, year triangulated from two age records).

## Open confirmation step
The **1850 and 1860 U.S. Federal Census** (Frederick A. Hubbell household, Champlain, Clinton Co., NY) would pin the exact year; both were login-gated in this run. Nothing in the free record contradicts ~1837.
