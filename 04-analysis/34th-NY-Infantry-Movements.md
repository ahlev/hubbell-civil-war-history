# 34th New York Infantry Regiment — Complete Chronological Movements (1861-1862)

**Also known as:** "The Herkimer Regiment"
**Composition:** 5 companies from Herkimer County, 2 from Steuben, 1 from Albany, 1 from Clinton, 1 from Essex County
**Colonel:** William La Due (resigned March 20, 1862); succeeded by James A. Suiter
**Enlistment:** Two years

---

## Brigade/Division Assignments (chronological)

| Date | Assignment |
|------|-----------|
| Aug 4, 1861 | Stone's Brigade, Division of the Potomac |
| Oct 15, 1861 | 2nd (Gorman's) Brigade, Stone's Division, Army of the Potomac |
| Mar 13, 1862 | Same brigade, Sedgwick's Division, 2nd Corps, Army of the Potomac |
| May 1862 | 1st Brigade, 2nd Division, 2nd Corps, Army of the Potomac |

**Sister regiments in Gorman's Brigade:** 1st Minnesota, 15th Massachusetts, 82nd New York

---

## ORGANIZATION & MUSTERING (May-June 1861)

- **May 1, 1861**: Mustered into New York State service at Albany
- **May 23, 1861**: State Board approved formation as Regiment No. 34
- **May 24, 1861**: Election of field officers ordered
- **May 30, 1861**: Officers confirmed — Col. William La Due, Lt. Col. James A. Suiter, Maj. Byron Laflin
- **June 15, 1861**: Mustered into U.S. service at Industrial School Barracks, Albany, NY
  - Location: Albany, NY (~42.6526°N, 73.7562°W)

---

## MOVEMENT TO WASHINGTON (July 1861)

- **July 3, 1861**: Departed Albany for Washington via New York, Philadelphia, Baltimore
- **July 5, 1861**: Arrived Washington, D.C.
  - Location: Washington, D.C. (~38.9072°N, 77.0369°W)
- **July 7, 1861**: Assigned camp on **Kalorama Heights**
  - Location: Kalorama Heights, Washington, D.C. (~38.9210°N, 77.0480°W)
  - *[Henry Hubbell letter: July 6, 1861, Washington, D.C.]*
- **July 20-21, 1861**: Ordered to Virginia; exchanged muskets for Enfield rifles
  - *[Henry Hubbell letter: July 21, 1861, Washington, D.C. — hears Bull Run]*
  - Note: The 34th was NOT engaged at First Bull Run; they remained near Washington

---

## SENECA MILLS / GREAT FALLS — Picket Duty (July-October 1861)

- **July 28, 1861**: Ordered to Seneca Mills
- **July 29, 1861**: Arrived **Great Falls**, MD; Companies B, G, and others detached for picket duty
  - Location: Great Falls, MD (~39.0001°N, 77.2536°W)
- **July 31, 1861**: Remaining companies arrived **Seneca Mills**; established **"Camp Jackson"**
  - Location: Seneca Mills, Montgomery County, MD (39°4.768'N, 77°20.413'W)
  - Near intersection of River Road and Seneca Creek, along C&O Canal
- **Aug 4, 1861**: Assigned to Stone's Brigade, Division of the Potomac
- **Aug-Oct 1861**: Picket duty along Potomac River and C&O Canal, covering 17-mile district
  - Occasional skirmishing; casualties included:
    - Sept 1: Pvt. William R. Bailey mortally wounded at Seneca Mills
    - Sept 16: Pvt. Oliver P. Darling killed at Seneca Mills
    - Oct 4: Lt. James R. Carr died at Seneca Mills
  - *[Henry Hubbell letters Sept-Oct 1861: "Washington, D.C. area" — actually at Seneca Mills, MD, about 25 miles NW of D.C.]*

---

## BALL'S BLUFF & POOLESVILLE (October 1861)

- **Oct 15, 1861**: Reassigned to Gorman's 2nd Brigade, Stone's Division
- **Oct 21, 1861**: Marched toward Poolesville; diverted to **Edwards Ferry**; crossed Potomac in scow boats
  - Location: Edwards Ferry, MD (~39.1247°N, 77.4486°W)
  - Arrived too late for Battle of Ball's Bluff; assisted removing wounded
- **Oct 23, 1861**: Recrossed to Maryland side; marched to **Poolesville**
  - Location: Poolesville, MD (39°8.777'N, 77°25.076'W)

---

## CAMP McCLELLAN — Winter Quarters (Oct 1861 - Feb 1862)

- **Oct 23, 1861 - Feb 24, 1862**: Established **"Camp McClellan"** at Poolesville, MD
  - Location: Poolesville, MD (39°8.777'N, 77°25.076'W)
  - Activities: Picketing the Potomac, building blockhouses, drilling
  - Part of Gen. Charles P. Stone's Corps of Observation (~20,000 troops in area)
  - *[Henry Hubbell letters: Nov 1861 - Feb 1862, "Camp McClellan" — CONFIRMED: Poolesville, MD]*

---

## HARPERS FERRY & SHENANDOAH VALLEY (Feb-March 1862)

- **Feb 24, 1862**: Orders received to move to Harpers Ferry
- **Feb 27, 1862**: Arrived **Harpers Ferry**; quartered in former cotton factory
  - Location: Harpers Ferry, WV (~39.3249°N, 77.7291°W)
- **Mar 3, 1862**: Moved to **Bolivar Heights**
  - Location: Bolivar Heights, WV (~39.3200°N, 77.7450°W)
  - *[Henry Hubbell letter: Feb 5, 1862 — "Bolivar Heights / Harpers Ferry"]*
  - *Note: Henry's Feb 5 date is BEFORE the regiment's Feb 24 departure from Poolesville per official records. This suggests either the regiment moved earlier than stated, or Henry was on detached duty, or the official date represents the main body's movement.*
- **Mar 9, 1862**: Moved to **Charlestown** (now Charles Town, WV)
  - Location: Charles Town, WV (~39.2890°N, 77.8596°W)
- **Mar 10, 1862**: Moved to **Berryville**, VA
  - Location: Berryville, VA (~39.1518°N, 77.9822°W)
- **Mar 12-13, 1862**: Marched toward **Winchester** (supporting Shields' operations)
  - Location: Winchester, VA (~39.1857°N, 78.1633°W)
- **Mar 14, 1862**: Marched back to Charlestown; established **"Camp Sedgwick"**
  - *[Henry Hubbell letter: March 1862, "Harpers Ferry" — regiment was moving between Harpers Ferry, Charlestown, and Berryville]*
- **Mar 15, 1862**: Formally attached to 1st Brigade, 2nd Division, 2nd Corps, Army of the Potomac
- **Mar 20, 1862**: Col. La Due resigned; Lt. Col. Suiter promoted to Colonel; Maj. Laflin to Lt. Col.; Capt. Charles L. Brown to Major

---

## MOVEMENT TO THE PENINSULA (March 1862)

- **Mar 22, 1862**: Crossed Potomac at Harpers Ferry on pontoon bridge; marched to **Sandy Hook**, MD
  - Location: Sandy Hook, MD (~39.3224°N, 77.7268°W)
- **Mar 23, 1862**: Reached **Washington, D.C.** at 2:00 AM; quartered behind Capitol
- **Mar 26, 1862**: Left Washington at 5:00 PM
- **Mar 27, 1862**: Reached **Alexandria**, VA (morning)
  - Location: Alexandria, VA (~38.8048°N, 77.0469°W)
- **Mar 29, 1862**: Embarked on transport **"Richard Willing"**
- **Mar 31, 1862**: Reached **Fortress Monroe**
  - Location: Fort Monroe, VA (~37.0054°N, 76.3083°W)

---

## PENINSULA CAMPAIGN — Yorktown Siege (April-May 1862)

- **Apr 1, 1862**: Landed at **Hampton**, VA at 1:00 PM
  - Location: Hampton, VA (~37.0299°N, 76.3452°W)
  - *[Henry Hubbell letter: April 2, 1862, "Hampton, VA"]*
- **Apr 4, 1862**: Reached **Big Bethel**
  - Location: Big Bethel, VA (~37.0944°N, 76.4256°W)
- **Apr 5, 1862**: Reached **Wynn's Mill** (also "Winne's Mills"); halted 2 miles from enemy outposts
  - Location: near Yorktown, VA (~37.2400°N, 76.5100°W)
  - *[Henry Hubbell letters: April 6-21, 1862, "Near Yorktown"]*
- **Apr 5 - May 4, 1862**: **SIEGE OF YORKTOWN**
  - Digging ditches, building entrenchments, skirmishing with enemy
  - Location: Yorktown siege lines, VA (~37.2367°N, 76.5098°W)
- **Apr 11, 1862**: Advanced nearer to enemy works at Yorktown

---

## PENINSULA CAMPAIGN — Yorktown to West Point (May 1862)

- **May 4, 1862**: Enemy evacuated; 34th NY **first regiment to enter enemy positions** at Wynn's Mill
  - *[Henry Hubbell letters: May 1-6, 1862, "Yorktown/Williamsburg"]*
- **May 5, 1862**: Marched to Yorktown; camped on heights
  - Location: Yorktown, VA (~37.2367°N, 76.5098°W)
  - Note: Battle of Williamsburg fought this day (May 5); 34th not directly engaged
- **May 6-7, 1862**: Moved to Yorktown Landing; embarked on schooner **"William"**
- **May 9, 1862**: Reached **Eltham** (Eltham's Landing) on the Pamunkey River
  - Location: Eltham, VA (~37.4500°N, 76.8200°W)
  - *[Henry Hubbell letters: May 9-12, 1862, "West Point, VA" — Eltham Landing is near West Point]*
  - West Point, VA location: (~37.5314°N, 76.7955°W)

---

## PENINSULA CAMPAIGN — Advance on Richmond (May 1862)

- **May 15, 1862**: Marched to **New Kent Court House**
  - Location: New Kent, VA (~37.5170°N, 76.9789°W)
- **May 18, 1862**: Moved toward **Cumberland Landing**
  - Location: Cumberland Landing, VA (~37.5200°N, 76.9000°W)
- **May 21, 1862**: Moved to **Bottom's Bridge** (crossing the Chickahominy)
  - Location: Bottom's Bridge, VA (~37.4800°N, 77.1700°W)
- **May 23, 1862**: Moved to **Tyler's House** on Beaver Creek (Beaver Dam Creek)
  - Location: near Mechanicsville, VA (~37.5500°N, 77.3500°W)
  - May 24: Pvt. Nicholas Moses (Co. H) mortally wounded at Tyler House

---

## BATTLE OF FAIR OAKS / SEVEN PINES (May 31 - June 1, 1862)

- **May 31, 1862**: Reached Fair Oaks battlefield; reinforced General Casey's broken line
  - **Led the advance in a charge**
  - Casualties: 29 enlisted killed/mortally wounded; Capt. Wells Sponable + 4 officers + 60 enlisted wounded; 3 missing/captured
  - Location: Fair Oaks, VA (~37.5100°N, 77.3000°W)
  - *[Henry Hubbell letters: June 1862, "Near Richmond"]*
- **June 1, 1862**: Engaged again; 2 killed, 4 wounded
- **June 16, 1862**: Pvt. Richard Mosher killed at White House (Landing)

---

## SEVEN DAYS BATTLES (June 25 - July 1, 1862)

- **June 27, 1862**: Skirmished with enemy entire day
  - *[Henry Hubbell letter: June 22 onward, "Near Richmond"]*
- **June 28, 1862**: Moved at 4:00 AM, covering rear to **Peach Orchard Station** (Allen's Farm/Savage Station area)
- **June 29, 1862**: Crossed **White Oak Swamp Bridge** at 4:00 AM; engaged at **Savage Station**
  - Location: Savage Station, VA (~37.4800°N, 77.2700°W)
- **June 30, 1862**: Engaged at **Glendale** (Frayser's Farm / White Oak Swamp), supporting Gen. Kearny
  - Casualties: 5 killed/mortally wounded; Lt. Walton, Lt. Northrup + 18 enlisted wounded; 1 officer + 34 enlisted captured
  - Location: Glendale, VA (~37.4300°N, 77.2500°W)
  - Arrived **Malvern Hill** after one hour's rest
- **July 1, 1862**: **BATTLE OF MALVERN HILL**
  - Casualties: Major Brown + Sgt. George Morse mortally wounded; total 34 officers/men killed, wounded, missing
  - Location: Malvern Hill, VA (~37.3850°N, 77.2470°W)
  - Capt. John Beverly promoted to Major

---

## HARRISON'S LANDING (July-August 1862)

- **July 2, 1862**: Retired to **Harrison's Landing**
  - Location: Harrison's Landing / Berkeley Plantation, VA (~37.3500°N, 77.1900°W)
  - *[Henry Hubbell letters: June 22 - July 28, 1862, "Near Richmond / Harrison's Landing"]*
- **July - Aug 14, 1862**: Camp duty at Harrison's Landing
- **Aug 4, 1862**: Reconnaissance to **Malvern Hill**; 1 killed, 3 wounded

---

## GAP: HARRISON'S LANDING TO ANTIETAM (Aug 15 - Sept 17, 1862)

**This is the critical movement gap — how they got from the Peninsula to Antietam:**

- **Aug 15, 1862**: Struck tents; began march to **Newport News**
- **Aug 21, 1862**: Arrived **Newport News**, VA
  - Location: Newport News, VA (~36.9787°N, 76.4280°W)
- **Aug 23, 1862**: Embarked on steamer **"Mississippi"** (with 15th MA and 1st MN) for Aquia Creek
- **Aug 25, 1862**: Landed at **Alexandria**, VA; marched to **Fort Corcoran** (Arlington)
  - Location: Fort Corcoran, Arlington, VA (~38.8950°N, 77.0700°W)
- **Aug 26, 1862**: Ordered to front; marched 8 miles then returned (2nd Bull Run crisis)
- **Aug 27, 1862**: Ordered to **Chain Bridge**; marched all night
- **Aug 28, 1862**: Arrived **Fort Ethan Allen** at daylight
  - Location: Fort Ethan Allen, Arlington, VA (~38.9100°N, 77.1300°W)
- **Aug 30, 1862**: Ordered to cover **Gen. Pope's retreat** from Second Bull Run
- **Sept 1, 1862**: Reached **Centreville**, VA; took rear guard position
  - Location: Centreville, VA (~38.8401°N, 77.4289°W)
- **Sept 4, 1862**: Returned to Chain Bridge; crossed into Maryland; encamped at **Tenallytown** (Tenleytown, now part of D.C.)
  - Location: Tenallytown, D.C. (~38.9470°N, 77.0790°W)

### Maryland Campaign March (Sept 7-16, 1862):

- **Sept 7, 1862**: 2nd Corps began march
- **Sept 8, 1862**: Passed through **Rockville**, MD
  - Location: Rockville, MD (~39.0840°N, 77.1528°W)
- **Sept 9, 1862**: Moved to **Middleburg** (Middlebrook?), MD
- **Sept 10, 1862**: Moved to **Clarksburg**, MD
  - Location: Clarksburg, MD (~39.2388°N, 77.2785°W)
- **Sept 12, 1862**: Moved to **Urbana**, MD
  - Location: Urbana, MD (~39.3261°N, 77.3414°W)
- **Sept 13, 1862**: Moved through **Frederick City**; continued to **Turner's Pass** (South Mountain)
  - Location: Frederick, MD (~39.4143°N, 77.4105°W)
  - Location: Turner's Gap, South Mountain (~39.4700°N, 77.6200°W)
- **Sept 14, 1862**: Arrived **Middletown**, MD; sent to picket at **Crampton's Gap** entrance
  - Location: Middletown, MD (~39.4437°N, 77.5445°W)
  - Location: Crampton's Gap (~39.4000°N, 77.6300°W)
- **Sept 15, 1862**: Corps moved to **Keedysville**, MD
  - Location: Keedysville, MD (~39.4853°N, 77.6984°W)
- **Sept 16, 1862**: Took position 1.5 miles southwest of Keedysville; bivouacked without shelter
  - Ordered to cook 4 days rations, prepare 40 rounds ammunition

---

## BATTLE OF ANTIETAM (September 17, 1862)

- **Sept 17, 1862 — 7:30 AM**: Left camp near Keedysville
  - Forded **Antietam Creek**
  - Marched westward into the **East Woods**
  - On extreme left of brigade line, facing west
  - Emerged from East Woods; became heavily engaged
  - Crossed open field and **Hagerstown Pike**
  - Entered **West Woods**; line extended north and south of **Dunker Church**
  - The 34th did NOT hear the order to veer right with Gorman's brigade; went left instead, toward the 125th Pennsylvania, engaging the 2nd South Carolina at close range
  - Left flank unprotected; danger of envelopment; hasty retreat
  - Reformed near the East Woods, organization intact
  - **CASUALTIES: 32 killed, 109 wounded, 9 missing — TOTAL 150 out of 311 engaged (48.2%)**
    - Among killed: Lt. Clarence Hill + 45 killed/mortally wounded total
    - Among wounded: Lt. Armineas Rounds + 96 enlisted
    - Among missing: Lt. John Kirk + 9 enlisted
  - **Henry Hubbell was killed in action this day**
  - Monument location: Confederate Avenue, ~110 yards west of Dunker Church (39°28'31.2"N, 77°44'52.9"W)

- **Sept 18, 1862**: Supported batteries on battlefield
- **Sept 19, 1862**: Buried the dead

- **Sept 29, 1862**: Marched to Bolivar Heights; went into camp

## KEY GAPS RESOLVED

### Gap 1: Washington to Seneca Mills (July 1861)
Henry's letters from "Washington, D.C." in July were written before/during the move. The regiment left Kalorama Heights on July 28 and was at Seneca Mills by July 31. Henry's "Washington, D.C." location in Sept-Oct 1861 was actually **Seneca Mills, MD** — about 25 miles northwest of D.C., along the Potomac and C&O Canal.

### Gap 2: Seneca Mills to Poolesville (October 1861)
After assisting at Ball's Bluff (Oct 21), the regiment established Camp McClellan at **Poolesville, MD** on October 23, 1861.

### Gap 3: Camp McClellan to Harpers Ferry (Feb 1862)
Departed Poolesville Feb 24, arrived Harpers Ferry Feb 27, 1862. Moved to Bolivar Heights March 3.
**NOTE:** Henry's letter dated Feb 5, 1862 from "Bolivar Heights" predates the official movement date of Feb 24. Possible explanations: advance party, detached duty, or variation in sources.

### Gap 4: Harpers Ferry to the Peninsula (March 1862)
Left Harpers Ferry March 22 → Sandy Hook → Washington (Mar 23) → Alexandria (Mar 27) → Transport "Richard Willing" (Mar 29) → Fort Monroe (Mar 31) → Hampton (Apr 1).

### Gap 5: Harrison's Landing to Antietam (Aug-Sept 1862)
Left Harrison's Landing Aug 15 → Newport News (Aug 21) → Steamer to Alexandria (Aug 23-25) → Fort Corcoran → Chain Bridge → covered Pope's retreat (Aug 30) → Centreville (Sept 1) → Tenallytown (Sept 4) → Maryland Campaign march through Rockville, Clarksburg, Urbana, Frederick, South Mountain → Keedysville (Sept 15) → Antietam (Sept 17).
