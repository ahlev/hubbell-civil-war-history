## Evidentiary Value

### Confirmed Facts
1. **Unit:** Co. H, 60th New York Infantry — matches all letter metadata
2. **Certificate No. 658,842** — matches "WC 658842" cited in Fred Jr.'s 1996 introduction
3. **Application No. 710,072** — matches pension reference in G10 genealogy notes
4. **Death date: December 7, 1894** — independently confirmed (also by gravestone in G11)
5. **Pension class: Invalid** — filed Dec 14, 1877, indicating disability claim (likely related to Lookout Mountain wound, minie ball left side, Dec 1, 1863)
6. **Discharge: Sept 14, 1865** — later than mustering out (Jun 27, 1865 per letters); may reflect administrative processing

### Death Date Resolution
This card provides the third independent confirmation of Alexander's death as **December 7, 1894** (not 1899):
- **M02 (this card):** "Died Dec. 7, 1894"
- **G10 (genealogy notes):** Pension record death date Dec 7, 1894
- **G11 (gravestone):** Confirms 1894 on Glenwood Cemetery stone

The "1899" date appearing in G03 (Emily's line) is the **widow's pension filing date**, not Alexander's death date.

### Wound-to-Pension Timeline
- **Dec 1, 1863:** Wounded at Lookout Mountain (minie ball, left side) — per LTR-1863-12-01-001
- **Jun 27, 1865:** Mustered out at Ogdensburgh — per LTR-1865-06-27-001
- **Dec 14, 1877:** Filed invalid pension application (12 years post-war)
- **Dec 7, 1894:** Died at Fonda, Iowa (age 50)
- **Post-1894:** Widow (Lois Wood Hubbell) filed dependent pension claim

## Notes

The 12-year gap between discharge and pension filing (1865→1877) suggests either delayed onset of disability symptoms or a change in pension law that made him eligible. The "Invalid" classification indicates a disability pension, distinct from service pensions available to all veterans.
