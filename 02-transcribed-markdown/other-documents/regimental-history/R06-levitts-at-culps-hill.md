# Levitt Family Visit to Culp's Hill, Gettysburg

## Full Transcription

### Slide 1

Levitt visit to exact spot on Culp's Hill, where the 60th New York fought on July 2, 1863 at Gettysburg and saved the Union right.

Culp's Hill had giant rock formations in the woods, which indicated the exact spot where Alexander Frederick Hubbell fought. Emily and Bruce decided to name the new arrival after him, hence Alexander Hubbell Levitt.

### Slide 2

Emily, Alex and Bruce visit Culps Hill at Gettysburg, where Alexander Frederick Hubbell fought with 60th NY in 1863.
