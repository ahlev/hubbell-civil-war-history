## Notes

- **CRITICAL DISCREPANCY — Alexander's death**: Gravestone reads "Died Dec. 7, 1894, AE. 50 Yrs." but Emily's line (G03) gives death as "Dec. 7, 1899" with application #710.072, Certificate #658.842. The December 7 date is consistent but the year differs: 1894 vs 1899. Born 1844, age 50 = 1894; age 55 = 1899. Cemetery transcription may be more reliable for the engraved year; the family document may have the correct year with official certificate numbers. Cross-reference with pension file (M02) needed. [data_quality: D]
- **CRITICAL DISCREPANCY — Julia's birthdate**: Gravestone gives Julia A. Hubbell "Born Mar. 28, 1844" — the SAME birthdate as Alexander (G01 says "born March 28, 1844"). Were they twins? Or is there an error? Julia died Jan 31, 1859, which matches G01 ("sister named Julia who died in 1859"). [data_quality: C — needs investigation]
- **Gravestone name**: The memorial stone reads "Alexander B. Hubbell" not "Alexander Fullerton Hubbell" — the "B" middle initial is unexplained and doesn't match any other document. [data_quality: D for middle name]
- The Civil War memorial was erected by the Sabbath School of the First Presbyterian Church of Champlain — indicating the family's church affiliation
- **Henry's regiment discrepancy**: Gravestone says Co. D, 34th Reg. NYV. But the 60th NY regimental history (R01) notes "BROTHER KILLED: Hubbell, Henry, 22, s,, Champlain, Antietam" in context of the 60th. The 1863 letters (L04) have Charles visiting Alexander at the 60th. Need to verify Henry's actual regiment. [data_quality: C]
- Three dates on the Julius C. Hubbell bronze plaque are noted as incorrect by the transcriber
- Jennie Christmas Hubbell lived to 101 years old (1877-1979)
- Cross-references: G01, G03, L04 (1863 letters), M01 (muster roll)
