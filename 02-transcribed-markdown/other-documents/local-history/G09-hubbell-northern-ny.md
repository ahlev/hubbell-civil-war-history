# G09 — Hubbell History of Northern NY Hubbells

## Transcription

**Source:** http://www.usgennet.org/usa/ny/state/northernnewyork/146-153.html

### Etymology

> HUBBELL. The Hubbell family is probably of Danish original and the name is believed to have been derived from "Hubba," a Danish chief, who settled in Britain in the ninth century, and the Anglo-Saxon word "hill." From "Hubba's hill" the name Hubhill was derived, and from that the surname Hubbell. The family is largely represented in all parts of the United States.

### Generation I: Richard Hubbell (b. 1627-28, Great Britain)

The first of the name in America. Born in Great Britain in 1627-28. Arrived in New England between 1645 and 1647. On March 7, 1647, he took the oath of fidelity to the government of the New Haven colony. Admitted a planter in Guilford, Connecticut, February 25, 1653-54. Lived there until 1664, when he and his family removed to Pequonnock, Fairfield County, Connecticut. Died October 23, 1699, aged about seventy-two years.

### Generation V: Wolcott Hubbell (b. 1754, Woodbury CT)

Son of Matthew Hubbell. Born in Woodbury, Connecticut, in 1754. When about fifteen years of age he removed with his father to Lanesborough, Massachusetts. He fought at the battle of Bennington, in the Revolution; was afterwards state senator, and for many years judge of one of the courts of Berkshire County.

**Married:** At age twenty, Mary, daughter of Major Thaddeus Curtis.

### Generation VI: Julius Caesar Hubbell (b. Apr 17, 1787, Lanesborough MA; d. Chazy NY, age 93)

Son of Wolcott Hubbell. Born at Lanesborough, Massachusetts, April 17, 1787, died at Chazy, New York, aged ninety-three years.

At the age of fifteen years he walked from Lanesborough, Massachusetts, to Burlington, Vermont; there took a boat and rowed to the mouth of Champlain River, now the Big Chazy River, and walked to Champlain, where his brother, Silas P. Hubbell, was practicing law. There he studied law with his brother, serving some seven years, the last three years doing most of the office business. He later walked from Champlain to Chazy, where he established himself in the profession of law, which he continued successfully until his death.

**Married:** Anna, daughter of Judge Pliny Moore, of Champlain, New York.

**Children:**

### Generation VII: John Wolcott Hubbell (b. Apr 28, 1830, Chazy NY)

Son of Julius Caesar Hubbell. Born at Chazy, New York, April 28, 1830. Educated in public schools of Chazy and at Keeseville, New York. Attended school at Williston Seminary, East Hampton, Massachusetts, and at Manchester, Vermont. Returned home to Chazy, engaged in farming. Assisted his father in his office for some time. Notary public for many years. Station agent for seven years, from 1877. Member of Grange, Patrons of Husbandry, and a Democrat in politics.

**Married:** Margaret Louise, born in Plattsburgh, New York, daughter of Judge George M. and Hannah Elizabeth (Mooers) Beckwith, the latter of whom was the daughter of General Benjamin Mooers.

**Children (all born in the house where he resides):**
