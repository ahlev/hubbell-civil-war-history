# G07 — Hubbell Genealogy, 1627–1889

## Source

A typed genealogical register tracing the Hubbell family through 8 generations from Richard Hubbell of Pequonnock (1627) to Alexander Fullerton Hubbell (1844). Three pages, clearly typed, likely compiled by a family researcher from published genealogies and family records.

---

## Full Transcription

### 1st Generation

**1627-8** — **RICHARD HUBBELL**, of Pequonnock, Fairfield Co., Colony of Conn, b. 1627-8 in Great Britain, d. [c.t.] 22, 1699, at his residence in Pequonnock. Married first; Elizabeth, daughter of John Weig, er. of New Haven Colony, Conn., in 1650-51. She died in 1654-5 in Pequonnock. Had issue: John, 1652, New Haven; RICHARD, 1654, Guilford, Conn.; James, 1656; Samuel, 1657, Guilford; Elizabeth, 1659, Guilford; Ebenezer, Mary and Martha, birth dates not recorded. Married second wife (christian name probably Ester or Elizabeth, for "E.H., 1689" is on gravestone in ancient Stratfield Burying Ground, Bridgeport, Conn.) Had issue: Samuel, born in Fairfield Co.; Abigail, b. in Fairfield; Sarah, Fairfield; James, Fairfield. Married third time to Mrs. Abigail Walker (widow of Joseph Walker of Stratford, Conn.) in 1688. She died in 1717. Had issue: Joseph, b. in Fairfield; John, b. in Fairfield Co.

### 2nd Generation

**1654** — **RICHARD HUBBELL**, Of Stratfield, Fairfield Co., Colony of Conn., born 1654, died 1738, son of Richard and Elizabeth Weig Hubbell. Married Rebecca, daughter of Samuel and Rebecca Morehouse, Nov. 5, 1684. She died April 2, 1695. Had issue: Ellis, born 1685; Ebenezer, 1687; Elizabeth, 1689; Jonathan, 1692. Married second wife, Hannah Sillway (or Silliman) of Malden, Mass., 1692. Had issue: Zachariah, 1695; Richard, 1696; Hannah, 1698; Eleazer, 1700; Nathaniel, 1702; Margery, 1704; Abigail, 1709.

### 3rd Generation

**1686** — **PETER HUBBELL**, of Newtown, Fairfield Co., Colony of Conn., born 1686, died 1780, son of Richard and Rebecca Morehouse Hubbell. Married first Katherine Wheeler, of Stratfield Parish, Fairfield Co., Conn., in 1709 in "Stratfield Parish". She died in 1742 in her 49th year. Had issue: Ephraim, born 1712; Peter, 1713; Ezra, 1717; Sarah, 1719; Jedidiah, 1720; MATTHEW, 1723; Gideon, 1726; Comfort, 1729; Katherine, 1732; Enoch, 1735; Silas, 1738. Married Sarah, who died in 1780. Had issue: Rhode, 1745; Mary, 1748; Phebe, 1749.

### 4th Generation

**1723** — **MATTHEW HUBBELL**, of Lanesborough, Berkshire Co., Mass., moved there from Woodbury, Conn., about 1770. Born 1723, son of Peter and Katharine Wheeler Hubbell. Married Abigail Hawley in 1743. Had issue: Ann, b. 1744 in Newton, Conn.; Hawley, 1746; Silas, supposed to have died in the Revolution; WOLCOTT, b. 1754 in Woodbury; Matthew, b. 1762; Sarah Clevin, b. 1764. Was a captain. Moved to Mass. in 1770.

### 5th Generation

**1754** — **WOLCOTT HUBBELL**, Of Lanesborough, Berkshire Co., Mass., sons of Matthew Hubbell and Abigail Hawley Hubbell. When 15, moved with his father Captain Matthew Hubbell to Lanesborough. At the age of 20 married Mary Curtis, daughter of Major Thaddeus Curtis, also a native of Woodbury. Had issue: SILAS; Wolcott; Julius Caesar, 1781; Loring Curtis, b. 1793; Algernon Sidney, b. about 1800; Sarah Hunter, 1792; Elizabeth; Mary; Nancy. Fought at **Battle of Bennington** in Revolutionary War. Was afterwards State Senator for Massachusetts, and for many years Judge of one of the Courts of Berkshire County. Died in 1839.

### 6th Generation

**Around 1783** — **SILAS HUBBELL**, Of Champlain, Clinton Co., N. Y. (no birth date given), died at age of 83 years. Son of Wolcott and Mary Curtis Hubbell. Married first Sarah Henderson. Had issue: **FREDERICK AUGUSTUS**, b. 1803; Silas, died before 1832; George W., died age 11 years; Henderson, died age 32 years; Juliana, married Charles Fullerton of Chicago, for who Fullerton Ave. is named and who left the Fullerton Art Collection to the Chicago Museum) (It was through this marriage that the "Hill" estate was left.). Married second time, Ruth Paddock. Had issue: Silas F., who lived in Champlain in 1880; Algernon Sidney Jones, died in infancy; Thomas, died in infancy. Was a member of Protestant Bible Society in Champlain and was instrumental in distributing Bibles among the French Canadians. (See Doulling's History of Rouselton, pg. 613.)

(Algernon Sidney Hubbell was born in about 1800 in the old homestead at Lanesborough, Mass., which has been standing more than one hundred and fifty years (or within of book few which the notes were been published in 1881) and is still in the possession of the family. He became a law partner of Gov. Briggs of Mass. Married Julia Jackson of Passaic, N. J., and removed to Newark and was engaged in the practice of law.)

## Key Data Extracted

### Critical Discovery: Julia Anna — Alexander's Twin Sister

Julia Anna Hubbell was Alexander's **twin sister**, born March 28/29, 1844. She died in January 1859, "in her 15th year" — two years before the war began. This resolves the mystery flagged in G11 (cemetery records) about Julia having the same birthdate as Alexander.

### Birth Order of the Six Children (Corrected)

The genealogy lists the children in this order, with birth dates:
1. **Frances McNeil Hubbell** — b. April 1837 (oldest, ~16 when father died)
2. **Henry B. Hubbell** — b. November 1839 (age ~22 at Antietam)
3. **Charles Fullerton Hubbell** — b. April 1840 (age ~22 at enlistment)
4. **James R. Hubbell** — b. January 1843 (youngest brother)
5. **Alexander Fullerton Hubbell** — b. March 28/29, 1844
6. **Julia Anna Hubbell** — b. March 28/29, 1844 (twin, died Jan 1859)

**Note:** The G05 Flickinger article calls Alexander "the fourth son" — but the genealogy lists him as fourth among the sons (Henry, Charles, James, Alexander). Henry was actually born before Charles based on the dates here.

### Alexander's Birth Date Discrepancy
- G07 says "Mch. 29, 1844" (page 3, 7th gen entry)
- G07's 8th gen entry says "Mch. 28, 1844"
- Gravestone (G05, G11) says "MAR. 28, 1844"
- Letters use March 28 as birthday
- **Resolution:** March 28, 1844 is correct. The "29" in the 7th gen entry is likely a typo.

### Mother's Maiden Name — Fully Resolved
**Frances (Farnell) McNeil** — The "(Farnell)" suggests her maiden name before marriage to Charles McNeil was Farnell. So:
- Born: Frances Farnell
- First married: Charles McNeil → became Frances McNeil
- Second married: Frederick Augustus Hubbell → became Frances McNeil Hubbell
- The "L." in "Frances L. (McNeil) Hubbell" (G05) may stand for a middle name, or the initial of her maiden name.

### Lois's Death Date Discrepancy
- G07 says "died Dec. 1922 at Mt. Dora, Fla." (8th gen entry)
- W01 (memorial) says "Dec. 30, 1927, Palmetto FL"
- W01 is a published memorial pamphlet from 1928 — more reliable
- **Resolution:** December 30, 1927 is likely correct. The 1922 date and "Mt. Dora" may be errors in this genealogy.

### Fullerton Connection (6th Generation)
Silas Hubbell's daughter **Juliana** married **Charles Fullerton of Chicago** — "for who Fullerton Ave. is named and who left the Fullerton Art Collection to the Chicago Museum." Alexander's middle name "Fullerton" comes from this family connection. Charles Fullerton Hubbell (brother) was also named for this branch.

### Wolcott Hubbell — Revolutionary War Hero (5th Generation)
**Wolcott Hubbell** (1754-1839) — fought at the **Battle of Bennington** (Aug 16, 1777). Later became State Senator for Massachusetts and judge. The family's military service tradition spans from the Revolution through the Civil War to WWI.
