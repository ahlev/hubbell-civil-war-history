### Generation 2: Alexander Fullerton Hubbell & Lois Ann Wood

**Alexander Fullerton Hubbell**
- b: March 28, 1844, Champlain, Clinton County, NY
- d. Dec. 7, 1899, app #710.072, Certif #658.842
- Buried: Cedar Cemetery, Fonda, Iowa
- 1860 census: Age 16, Champlain NY

**Civil War service — 60th New York Infantry, Co. H:**
- Enlisted Sept. 21, 1861 (received bounty of $166)
- Promoted Oct. 21, 1862 to Sergeant
- Reenlisted Dec. 14, 1863
- Mustered out July 7, 1865 in Alexandria, Va.
- Official records: Gettysburg, Antietam, Eight Days Dallas, Kennesaw, Atlanta and others
- Lookout Mountain... wounded.

**Post-war:**
After war, horseback Fort Dodge to Dover Township, Iowa, purchased all of Section 26. Prominent in the township and held many official offices.

**Married:** Lois Ann Wood, April 1, 1887, Fonda, Iowa [?r.e. fliekinger pasyor?] [ed: possibly "Rev. Flickinger, pastor"]
- Lois daughter of A. B. P. Wood
- 1900 census: Cedar Falls City, Blackhawk County, Iowa... born Wisconsin 1857
- Lois was graduate of the Darlington high school and first principal in the independent district of Fonda, 1880

**Children:**
1. Frederick Alexander — Jan. 4, 1888 [ed: compiler notes "Augustus vs Alexander???"]
2. Affa — June 1889
3. Wolcott Wood — Oct. 24, 1890
4. Helen — April 1892

## Notes

- Pages 5-9 of the source PDF contained embedded images (addresses, buildings, photos) with no extractable text. The DOCX source (G04) likely contains these images.
- **Name confusion**: Compiler questions "Augustus vs Alexander???" for the first son. G01 calls him "Frederic Augustus" while this document and later records use "Frederick Alexander." The Gen 10/11 naming is tangled — needs resolution. [data_quality: C]
- **Key narrative detail**: Alexander Hubbell Levitt was named at Gettysburg battlefield, at Culp's Hill, at the exact site of the 60th NY memorial where his ancestor fought. This is a powerful family tradition linking to R01-R08.
- **Death date 1899 vs 1894**: This document gives Alexander's death as Dec 7, 1899 with official application and certificate numbers. The cemetery stone (G11) gives 1894. The certificate numbers lend authority to 1899. [data_quality: B for 1899, supported by official record numbers]
- **Marriage officiant**: "[?r.e. fliekinger pasyor?]" likely reads "Rev. Flickinger, pastor" — suggesting a church wedding. [data_quality: D]
- **Lois born Wisconsin 1857**: This is the only document giving Lois's birthplace and approximate year. Cross-reference with Wood family documents (W01-W10).
