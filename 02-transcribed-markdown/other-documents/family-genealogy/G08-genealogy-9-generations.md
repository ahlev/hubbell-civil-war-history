# G08 — Hubbell Genealogy, 9 Generations (1627–1889+)

## Source

A 4-page document consisting of a typed cover page (added by a family researcher) followed by the same 3-page typed genealogy that appears as G07, with the addition of a 9th generation entry for Frederick Alexander Hubbell (Alexander the soldier's son). The cover page extends the lineage forward through generations 10–13 to the present-day custodian.

### 1st Generation

**1627-8** — **RICHARD HUBBELL:** Of Paquonnock, Fairfield Co., Colony of Conn. b. 1627-8 in Great Britain, d. [c.t.] Oct. 23, 1699, at his residence in Pequonnock. Married first Elizabeth, daughter of John Weig, er. of New Haven Colony, Conn., in 1650-51. She died in 1654-5 in Pequonnock. Had issue: John, 1652, New Haven; RICHARD, 1654, Guilford, Conn; James, 1656; Samuel, 1657, Guilford; Elizabeth, 1659, Guilford; Ebenezer, Mary and Martha, birth dates not recorded. Married second wife (christian name probably Ester or Elizabeth, for "E.H., 1688" is on gravestone in ancient Stratfield Burying Ground, Bridgeport, Conn.). Had issue: Samuel, born in Fairfield Co.; Abigail, b. in Fairfield; Sarah, Fairfield; James, Fairfield. Married third time to Mrs. Abigail Walker (widow of Joseph Walker of Stratford, Conn.) in 1688. She died in 1717. Had issue: Joseph, b. in Fairfield; John, b. in Fairfield Co.

### 2nd Generation

**1654** — **RICHARD HUBBELL:** Of Stratfield, Fairfield Co., Colony of Conn., born 1654, died 1738, son of Richard and Elizabeth Weigs Hubbell. Married Rebecca, daughter of Samuel and Rebecca Morehouse, Nov. 5, 1685. She died April 2, 1692. Had issue: PETER, born 1686; Ebenezer, 1687; Elizabeth, 1689; Jonathan, 1692. Married second wife, Hannah Sillway (or Silliman) of Malden, Mass., 1692. Had issue: Zachariah, 1695; Richard, 1696; Hannah, 1698; Eleazer, 1700; Nathaniel, 1702; Margery, 1704; Abigail, 1709.

### 3rd Generation

**1686** — **PETER HUBBELL:** Of Newtown, Fairfield Co., Colony of Conn., born 1686, died 1780, son of Richard and Rebecca Morehouse Hubbell. Married first Katherine Wheeler, of Stratfield Parish, Fairfield Co., Conn. in 1709 in "Stratfield Parish". She died in 1742 in her 49th year. Had issue: Ephraim, born 1712; Peter, 1715; Ezra, 1717; Sarah, 1719; Jedidiah, 1720; MATTHEW, 1723; Gideon, 1726; Comfort, 1729; Katharine, 1732; Enoch, 1735; Silas, 1738. Married Sarah, who died in 1780. Had issue: Rhode, 1745; Mary, 1748; Phebe, 1749.

### 4th Generation

**1723** — **MATTHEW HUBBELL**, of Lanesborough, Berkshire Co., Mass., moved there from Woodbury, Conn., about 1770. Born 1723, son of Peter and Katharine Wheeler Hubbell. Married Abigail Hawley in 1743. Had issue: Ann, b. 1744 in Newton, Conn.; Hawley, 1746; Silas, supposed to have died in the Revolution; WOLCOTT, b. 1754 in Woodbury; Matthew, b. 1762; Sarah Clevin, b. 1764. Was a captain. Moved to Mass. in 1770.

### 5th Generation

**1754** — **WOLCOTT HUBBELL:** Of Lanesborough, Berkshire Co., Mass., sone [sic] of Matthew Hubbell and Abigail Hawley Hubbell. When 15, moved with his father Captain Matthew Hubbell to Lanesborough. At age of 20 married Mary Curtis, daughter of Major Thaddeus Curtis, also a native of Woodbury. Had issue: SILAS; Wolcott; Julius Caesar, 1787; Loring Curtis, b. 1798; Algernon Sidney, b. about 1800; Sarah Eunice, 1782; Elizabeth; Mary; Nancy. Fought at Battle of Bennington in Revolutionary War. Was afterwards State Senator for Massachusetts, and for many years Judge of one of the Courts of Berkshire County. Died in 1839.

### 6th Generation

**Around 1783** — **SILAS HUBBELL:** Of Champlain, Clinton Co., N. Y. (no birth date given), died at age of 83 years. Son of Wolcott and Mary Curtis Hubbell. Married first Sarah Henderson. Had issue: **FREDERICK AUGUSTUS**, b. 1803; Silas, died before 1820; George W., died age 11 years; Henderson, died age 24 years. Juliana, married Charles Fullerton of Chicago, for who Fullerton Ave. is named and who left the Fullerton Art Collection to the Chicago Museum. (It was through this marriage that the "Hill" estate was left.) Married second time, Ruth Paddock. Had issue Silas P., who lived in Champlain in 1880; Algernon Sidney Jones, died in infancy; Thomas, died in infancy. Was a member of Protestant Bible Society in in Champlain and was instrumental in distributing Bibles among the French Canadians. (See Dowling's History of Romanism, pg. 613.)

(Algernon Sidney Hubbell was born in about 1800 in the old homestead at Lanesborough, Mass. which has been standing more than one hundred and fifty years (as of editing of book from which these notes were taken published in 1881) and is still in the possession of the family. He became a law partner of Gov. Briggs of Mass. Married Julia Jackson of Passaic, N. J. and removed to Newark and was engaged in the practice of law.)

### 7th Generation

**1803** — **FREDERICK AUGUSTUS HUBBELL:** Of Champlain, Clinto [sic] Co., N. Y. son of Silas and Sarah Henerson [sic] Hubbell. Born in Champlain in 1803. For twenty years an eminent member of the Bar. Died April 25, 1853 in Champlain. Married Frances(l) McNeil, daughter of Charles McNeil of Charlotte, Vermont in 1833. (Description of old McNeil Ferry properties in family scrapbook.) Had issue:

### 8th Generation

**1844** — **ALEXANDER FULLERTON HUBBELL:** Of Fonda, Pocahantas [sic] Co., Iowa, son of Frederick Augustus and Francis McNeil Hubbell, born in Champlain, Clinton Co., N. Y., Mch. 29, 1844. Married Lois Keyser Wood, second daughter of Alpheus Putnam and Cordelia Keyser Wood of near Fayette, LaFayette Co., Wisconsin and Fonda, Iowa. Born Mch. 24, 1857, died in 71st year on Dec. 30, 1927. Had issue: FREDERIC ALEXANDER, b. Jan. 4, 1889 at Fonda, Iowa; Affa Wood, b. June 1890 at Fonda; Woolcott [sic] Wood, b. Oct., 1891; Helen, b. at Fonda, April, 1893, died, Dec. 1929 at Mt. Dora, Fla.

### 9th Generation

**1889** — **FREDERICK ALEXANDER HUBBELL:** Of Atlanta, Fulton County, Ga. Son of Alexander Fullerton and Lois Wood Hubbell. Born in Fonda, Pocahantas [sic] County, Iowa on Jan. 4, 1889. Married Gladys Gibson Sands of Atlanta, Nov. 12, 1921. Gladys Sands Hubbell was daughter of William Gibson and Clodean Swan Sands of Atlanta. She was born Nov. 12, 1895 in Atlanta; died Mar. 2, 1966 in Atlanta. Had issue Frederick Alexander, Jr. b. Dec. 18, 1922 and Gledis Ann b. July 27, 1925. Frederick Alexander Hubbell graduated from Univ of Michigan at Ann Arbor in 1914. He served with the AEF, of the U. S. Army in France 1918-1919. He resided in Atlanta from 1920 until his death and was regarded as one of the leading structural engineers of that area.

### The "Died Dec. ___ at Mt. Dora" Mystery

Both G07 and G08 end the 8th generation entry with a death reference to Mt. Dora, Florida, but with different years:
- G07: "Died, Dec. 1922 at Mt. Dora, Fla."
- G08: "died, Dec. 1929 at Mt. Dora, Fla."

Neither matches known facts:
- Alexander died **Dec 7, 1894** in Cedar Falls, Iowa (triply confirmed by M02, G10, G11)
- Lois died **Dec 30, 1927** in Palmetto, Florida (confirmed by W01)

The "Dec. 1929" in G08 may refer to **Helen** (b. April 1893), the youngest child — the punctuation could place this death note with Helen rather than Alexander. If so, Helen Hubbell died December 1929 at Mt. Dora, Florida, at age 36. This would be new information not found elsewhere in the collection.

### Structural Engineer at "Tuscan Steel" — Cross-Reference

W01 (Lois memorial cover page) says Frederick Alexander became a "structural engineer at Tuscan Steel in Youngstown, OH, then to Knoxville." G08 says he "resided in Atlanta from 1920" and was a leading structural engineer. Timeline: Youngstown → Knoxville → Atlanta (by 1920).

## Notes

G08 is the same core genealogy as G07 with two additions: (1) a cover page extending the lineage to the present day, and (2) a 9th generation entry for Frederick Alexander Hubbell that provides substantial new biographical data about Alexander's eldest son — his education, WWI service, career, marriage, and children.

The cover page's notation "End of direct Hubbell line" after Emily Winn Hubbell (Gen 11) followed by "1988 Alexander Hubbell Levitt" is poignant — the Hubbell surname ended when Emily married Bruce Levitt, but the family named their son Alexander Hubbell Levitt, preserving the Civil War soldier's name across 12 generations and 144 years.

The 2nd generation correction from "Ellis" (G07) to "Peter" (G08) is significant — Peter is the correct name for the direct ancestor in the 3rd generation entry. G07's "Ellis" appears to be a transcription error that G08 corrects.
