# G10 — Genealogy Notes (DOC Version of Emily's Line)

## CRITICAL: Death Date Resolution

**Alexander Fullerton Hubbell died December 7, 1894 — NOT 1899.**

This document contains the key line: "Died according to pension: Dec. 7, 1894" followed by "Widow filing date: Dec. 12, 1899 app # 710.072 Certif #658.842"

The **1899 date** that appears in G03 (Emily's Line PDF) is the **widow's pension filing date**, not Alexander's death date. Previous transcriptions (G01, G03) conflated these two dates. The pension record clearly distinguishes:
- **Death:** December 7, 1894
- **Widow's filing:** December 12, 1899 (five years later)

This resolves the discrepancy with the G11 cemetery record, which gives Alexander's death year as 1894.
