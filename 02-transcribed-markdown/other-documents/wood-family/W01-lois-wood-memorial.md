### Mother's Maiden Name — New Information
**Cordelia (Keyser) Wood** — Lois's mother's maiden name was **Keyser**. This is the first document to identify her maiden name. Alpheus Putnam Wood married Cordelia Keyser.

## Notes

The memorial pamphlet's physical condition is poor — rotated 90° and heavily degraded in many areas. The cover page (typed by the family researcher, presumably Bruce Levitt) captures the essential biographical data clearly. A higher-quality scan or the original DOCX source (G06) might yield the full memorial text.

The pamphlet was copyrighted in Washington, D.C. (1928) — suggesting it was printed professionally, not just a local church bulletin. This indicates the family's stature and Lois's significance in the community.

Lois outlived Alexander by 33 years. She moved five times after his death — Cedar Falls, Madison, Ames, Palmetto — always prioritizing her children's education and opportunities. The memorial describes her as a woman of deep faith, educational leadership, and community service.
