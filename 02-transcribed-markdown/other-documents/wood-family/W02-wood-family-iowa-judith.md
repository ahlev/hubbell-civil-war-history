# W02 -- Wood Family in Iowa: Judith V. Wood Email (1997)

## Source Context

This is a 1997 Rootsweb ROOTS-L mailing list post by Judith V. Wood, a direct descendant of Abram Grosvenor Wood (Generation 9 in the Wood line). Judith received information from Darlene Goerner, president of the Pocahontas County Genealogy Society, Iowa, consisting of photocopied pages about her great-grandfather from local records.

This email serves as an **independent cross-reference** confirming the Hubbell-Wood connection: Lois Ann Wood married Alexander F. Hubbell. The information derives from a family researcher working from Pocahontas County sources, independently corroborating the published Pioneer History (W07) and the lineage notes (G01).

Hi there, I just received a photocopy of pages on my great grandfather from Darlene Goerner, president of the Pocahontas County Genealogy Society, Iowa. Some of my grandfather's siblings are listed below, and I wondered if any of you are connected. Also, she sent some Wood obits that are unrelated to mine as far as I can tell, but I recognize some of the names from people who have been on the WOOD-roots list. I will list them also

My grandfather, Abram Grosvenor WOOD's siblings:

Adele Wood, married on Oct 5, 1876 to William J. CURKEET, b. July 6, 1846, one daughter, Rena. They came from Darlington, WI to Pocahontas county, Grant township. Rena graduated from Iowa State Normal School in 1898, Cedar Falls, and went to Musical Observatory at Oberlin, Ohio. William died of an accicent and paralysis, Jan 12, 1884.

Lois Ann Wood, principal of school district of Fonda, IA in 1880, married Alexander F.HUBBELL

Adelbert Sylvanus Wood, b. Aug 25, 1878, cashier of the Pocahontas County Bank, Fonda. Married Mary Josephine, daughter of William and Abigail ALEXANDER. Children: Arthur Bowan, Lucian, Percy Eugene and Delphine.

Cora Estelle Wood married Dr. M.F. Patterson

Engenia and Affa died young


OTHER WOOD listings:

GEORGE WASHINGTON WOOD, b. Oct 25, 1825, resident of Cedar township from 1869 to 1892, a native of Warren county, N.Y. Married to: Sarah Reach, died Dec 24, 2879; one daughter marreied Nelson E. BAILEY. 2nd: Mary LOVEWELL, died at Fonda Jan 16, 1890.

JOHN MARTIN WOOD, b. Apr 3, 1822, d. Cedar Township, Jan 13, 1900. A native of Warren county, N>Y> July 1854 married Sarah T. TUBBS. They were real pioneers, and raised 10 children. names:Alice married Joseph GATON; Mary Maria married Julian ADAMS, Fonda; George W.WOOD and Charles C. WOOD; Wilson WOOD married Mary JENKINS; Lydia married Gus EIKHOFF, Fonda; John Elmer WOOD and Ezra Eugene WOOD; William married Emma HOLYER, Fonda, and Oller F. WOOD.

from obit. in FT Dodge Messenger, 11 Aug 1978

DEWEY A. WOOD: died at age 81, in Fonda. Survivors are dau.: Mrs. DeWayne (Virginia)BECKFIELD; Mrs. Rae NETWIG and Mrs. Jane ALLEN, all of Fonda. He was born in Calhoun Co., married Cora Lucinda MOULTON.

ARCHIE WOOD: Died at age 89. married to Genevieve Scroggins in 1924. Sons: LaVerne, Gary, Philip, ; dau. Mrs. Everett (Judy) Johnson, and sisters, Mrs. Fern Hummel, Geneva Kinyon, Esther Heller, Marguerite Holcomb, and Sarah Bradley.


There are the bare facts, as there was too much to transcribe right now.

Judith V. Wood

## Notes

- **Researcher annotation** (from raw extract header): "I forget how we got this from a distant relative, Judith V. Wood -- very early foray onto Ancestry?"
- This email independently confirms the Hubbell-Wood marriage: "Lois Ann Wood, principal of school district of Fonda, IA in 1880, married Alexander F. HUBBELL"
- The "Other Wood listings" section contains **unrelated** Wood families in Pocahontas County; George Washington Wood and John Martin Wood are from Warren County, NY but are not part of the Alpheus Wood line
- **Typo in source**: "Dec 24, 2879" should read Dec 24, 1879 (Sarah Reach death)
- **Typo in source**: "accicent" should read "accident"
- **Discrepancy**: Adelbert's birth year is given here as 1878 but in W07 (Pioneer History) as Aug. 25, 1858. The 1858 date is more consistent with the family timeline; 1878 appears to be a transcription error in the email
- Judith V. Wood is Generation 10 in the Wood line (see W06 for full genealogy)
