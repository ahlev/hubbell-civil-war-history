## Full Transcription

> June 22, 1864
> Morganza, La.
>
> Dear Mother:
>
> As James wrote you yesterday I will write a few lines also and send with him today. We have been waiting to hear from you before we wrote, but as no letter comes will wait no longer.
>
> Two or three days since a boy came to us and said that the new doctor wanted to see the two Hubbells. We went there and learned that Doctor James Sweeney was Assistant Surgeon of this Regt. He told us that he saw Frances about three weeks before. He was quite sociable. The Regt. has had but one surgeon since it left Washington till now. On the $400 we sent by express we paid $4 freight and two dollars insurance.
