## Full Transcription

> Oct. 12th, 1862
> Eckington Hospital
> Washington, D.C.
>
> Dear Alex:
>
> It is so long since I have seen or even heard from you that I hardly know whether you are alive or not. How do you get along? Do you have good health? I have had a pretty long pull off sickness but am getting well now fast. I expect to be able to return to the company before long.
>
> How are all the boys? There must have been some promotions lately, hasn't there? Do write and let me know who and what they are. It is so long since I have heard anything to speak of about matters in the Co. & Regt. that I would like to hear very much. Do you have much duty to do now where you are? I think I shall be able to stand it before long. I want to at any rate. Capt. Ransom was here to see me yesterday. Just from home and brought me some things that I was very much in need of. I was glad to see him looking so much better than he did the last time I saw him before.
>
> Now Alex do write immediately and tell me all the news.
>
> Hoping to see you soon, I remain as ever, your friend,
>
> Amos C. Luther
