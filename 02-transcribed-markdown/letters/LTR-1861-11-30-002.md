## Full Transcription

> Nov. 30th, 1861
> Camp McClellan
>
> Dear Frances:
>
> I was very glad to get a letter from you. I have been a little homesick lately, but I have got over it. I see you are forming plans for me when I go home. I hope they will turn out good. I should not wonder but they will, I hope so.
>
> I saw Brink not long since, said he had received a letter from you, seemed to be in good humor. I have not continued my Journals. Have not had much time. Shall continue them next week. Our Company have all got new blankets, very heavy and good.
>
> In regard to our Company suffering so mucy. It is not correct. There was one time in Virginia we had a pretty hard time, but that was all. We have a brigade drill two or three times a week, three Regts. of New York 2d and the Minnesota, besides ours. Gorman is General. I have nothing more to write at present. Write often.
>
> I remain your aff. brother,
>
> Henry
