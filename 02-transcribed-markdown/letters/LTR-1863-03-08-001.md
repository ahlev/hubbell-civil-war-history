## Full Transcription

> March 8, 1863
> Aquia Landing
>
> My dear Mother and Sister:
>
> I have written you two letters to your one, nevertheless, I shall write you again and expect the answers will come in due time. How do you get along now? Hope you had any sleigh rides since I left? Be careful, Miss Fannie, the next time you ride out not to scare any more poor Frenchmen as badly as we did those when coming from Cheesje. Poor chaps — wonder if they have recovered yet. Do not fail when you write to let me know how Charley and the fat Frenchman get along — if he still keeps him warm.
>
> We are still building fortifications and are having jolly times. We have a new Captain — Lieut. Delany of Co. F — a most excellent man — one after my own heart. Therefore in directing your letters direct to the care of Capt. Delany. I neglected to tell you that both of our Lieuts. have resigned and gone home.
>
> Goodrich, I received a letter a few days since from cousin Mary McNeil of Goodrich, Mich. It is quite a political dissertation and very amusing. I think I shall enclose it and send it to you. If I do, please put it with my package of old letters. I shall try and answer it today. Love to all.
>
> Yours affectionately,
>
> Alexander F. Hubbell
>
> P.S. You will enjoy Cousin Mary's letter up to "ninety nine."
