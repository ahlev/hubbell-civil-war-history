## Full Transcription

> March 26, 1862
> Camp Preston King
> near Baltimore
>
> My Dear Mother:
>
> You see by the heading of my letter that we have again "struck tents." Our Company is now at headquarters. We left our quarters on Eutaw St. last Saturday. It is rumored that our Regiment will leave within ten days for Fortress Monroe. Our Colones is now there. He has said, I understand, that if our Regt. is not moved off this road he will resign. He is like very much by all. He is a savage looking old fellow with a moustache and beard of a wolfish gray. He says but little, and what he says he means. I expect the call for drill every moment. Love to all.
>
> Your affectionate son,
>
> Alex F. Hubbell
>
> P.S. Direct your letters as usual. I am enjoying excellent health and spirits. Please write soon. You need not consider this as a letter, but only a dispatch to let you know that I am well and my whereabouts. I received a letter and paper from home the day we moved and one from Henry Monday. He writes in good spirits. Told me about their marching to Winchester and how the rebels took to their heels. They (the rebels) seem to have been drilling on the "double quick" of late. Why don't James write. I shall pull his ears when I get home, see if I don't.
