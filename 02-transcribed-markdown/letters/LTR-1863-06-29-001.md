## Full Transcription

> June 29, 1863
> Alexandria, Va.
>
> Dear Frances:
>
> I have received two papers from you since Mother left home. How do you get along alone and where are you staying? Received a very short letter from Mother a few days since which I answered. Suppose she is still at Charlotte, but
