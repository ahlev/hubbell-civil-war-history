## Full Transcription

> May 1st
>
> Dear Mother:
>
> I enclose twenty dollars by express. I was on picket today. There was heavy cannonading this morning about four o'clock. The rebel fort is to be seen from our camp. Write often.
>
> Your aff. Son,
>
> H.H.
