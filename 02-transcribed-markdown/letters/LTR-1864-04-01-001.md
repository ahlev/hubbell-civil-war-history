## Full Transcription

> April 1st, 1864
> Stevenson, Ala.
>
> Dear Mother:
>
> Have had old warrant renewed so that I now hold my old position in the 60th Veterans. It is merely a matter of form. Please preserve this one as you have the other. It is now some time since I heard from you. When the mail came in this morning and no letter for me I was very much disappointed. Please write soon. We have received marching orders and will leave in a few days for Chattanooga or Knoxville, probably.
>
> If you can send the boots direct them the same as the letters. Be careful and put on in care of Capt. Delaney. Love to all. Please write soon. Have you heard from James and Charles?
>
> Your aff. son,
>
> Alex F. Hubbell
>
> P.S. Please send me some stamps. I am entirely out and shall have to borrow to send this.
