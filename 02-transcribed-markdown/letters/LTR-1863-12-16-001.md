## Full Transcription

> Dec. 16th, 1863
> Wauhatchie, Tenn.
>
> Dear Mother:
>
> I am again with the regt. and doing duty. My side is still pretty sore but then I manage to get along first rate. My wound caused me to miss the fights of Missionary Ridge and Ringgold. I did not like the way things worked at the hospital and told the surgeon I was ready to go back to the regt. Two others of the regt. came back with me. We are back again in our old camp and we are pretty hard up for rations. The whole army is in the same way. We are on half rations. The railroad will be open in a few days and we shall have abundance soon.
>
> Old Bragg is pretty well used up and will be wiped out before long, I think. The Regt. was in two battles since I left — Missionary Ridge and Ringgold. Loss of Company H. is as follows: Doten and Michael Housedean, killed; Alfred Lemage and Thomas Small, wounded. Thos. Small had his leg torn off.
>
> I have only a small bit of candle left and must close. Give my regards to Capt. Ransom and let him know the loss of the Co. Love to all. Do not write until you hear from me again as we expect to make a move soon and I am afraid I would lose the letter.
>
> Hoping to see you all soon, I remain
>
> Your Aff. Son,
>
> Alex F. Hubbell
>
> P.S. Sergt. Long was not hurt.

## Editorial Notes

Alexander returns to his regiment early, against medical advice. "I did not like the way things worked at the hospital and told the surgeon I was ready to go back." His side is "still pretty sore" — a minie ball wound less than three weeks old — but he's doing duty. The army is on half rations, the railroad hasn't reopened, and he's writing by the last inch of a candle. This is the Western Theater's supply crisis in miniature.

The casualty list is now confirmed: at Lookout Mountain, Missionary Ridge, and Ringgold combined, Co. H lost Doten and Housedean killed, and Lemage, Small, Rider, and Alexander himself wounded. Small lost his leg. This is the final accounting of the Chattanooga Campaign's cost to Alexander's company.
