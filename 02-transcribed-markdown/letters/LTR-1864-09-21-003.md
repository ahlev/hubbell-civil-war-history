## Full Transcription

> Sept. 21, 1864
> U.S. Field Hospital No. 2
> Chattanooga, Tenn.
>
> Dear Fannie:
>
> I shall now only write an apology for a letter and shall conclude with a promise of a long letter before a great while, and I hope I shall find grace in your eyes. I do not feel very well today, and I trust you will regard this as a sufficient excuse for these few lines. I only write them to let you know I have not forgotten you. Please write me often.
>
> Your aff. Brother,
>
> Alex F. Hubbell
