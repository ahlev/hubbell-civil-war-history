## Full Transcription

> March 1st, 1862
>
> Dear Mother:
>
> I intended to have mailed this letter before but could not get a chance. I shall mail this as soon as I can. Today is rather cold but pleasant. We are now in a large brick building which used to be a grist mill. Some place where the rebles took so much wheat they will never have the same chance again. U gave not been around town and of course cannot write as long a letter as I should like. When I write again I may have something more to write. Write soon.
>
> Your Aff.
>
> Henry
