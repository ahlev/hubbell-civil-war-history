# Press & Outreach — Entry Point

The personalized media-outreach engine for the Hubbell Civil War Ancestry Project. **Research + drafts only — nothing here has been sent.**

## How this works
1. **Media kit** — the stable assets every pitch references. Start at `00-fact-sheet.md` + `MEDIA-KIT.md`; angles in `03-story-angles.md`.
2. **Dossiers** — one verified file per writer, in `dossiers/<category>/`. Each holds who they are, why they fit, the weighted way-in, a genuine personalization hook, their notable work, verified contact details, and a drafted pitch.
3. **Pitch drafts** — mirrored in `outreach-drafts/` (one per writer).
4. **Tracker** — `outreach-tracker.csv` logs every contact + status.

## Categories (≥30 writers each)
- `dossiers/01-local/` — Champlain/North-Country + Antietam/Sharpsburg/W-Maryland + North-Georgia/Atlanta/Athens
- `dossiers/02-history-museum/` — historical-interest & museum blogs/newsletters
- `dossiers/03-technology/` — technology blogs & publications
- `dossiers/04-major-history/` — larger/national historical publications

## Cardinal rule for this engine
**Filter, never a source — applied to journalists.** Every contact datum (email, employer, byline, "notable pieces") is verified against a live source and cited, or marked **UNVERIFIED**. Nothing is fabricated. Where no public email exists, the best verifiable channel is given; any inferred email pattern is flagged **INFERRED**, never presented as confirmed.
