Congratulations on taking the helm at NGS Magazine. Your *Story of My Life* workbook — helping people actually preserve and tell family legacy — is part of why I'm writing.

The Hubbells were a Champlain, New York family. Four brothers enlisted in 1861; they and their mother Frances wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed by a relative in 1947–49, digitized by my father, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published them as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers; the line settled in Iowa.

I'd love to write a how-to feature for NGS Magazine: the replicable steps — transcription, structuring, and verification — including how I kept identity honest with curated alias tables instead of fuzzy matching, and used AI strictly as a disciplined judge, a filter never a source. The throughline: what any member could do with their own box of letters.
