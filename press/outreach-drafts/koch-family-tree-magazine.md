Hi Andrew,

Your 2025 wrap-up on where genealogy is heading — full-text search, AI, all of it — is the conversation I've been living inside this year, so I wanted to bring you a concrete case.

My family kept 273 Civil War letters: four Hubbell brothers and their mother, Frances, writing home to Champlain, NY, 1861–1870. My great-grandmother typed them in the late 1940s; my father scanned them; this year I transcribed, structured, and published the whole correspondence, and cross-referenced it against public archives, including Library of Congress newspapers. I used AI throughout — but strictly as a filter, never a source: it organized and checked, it never invented, and every person was identified by hand, not fuzzy-matched.
