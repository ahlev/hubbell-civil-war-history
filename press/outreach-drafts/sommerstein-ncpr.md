**Subject:** A Champlain family's Civil War letters — good radio, in their own words

Hi David,

You've spent 25 years on North Country stories that turn on real people and real places, so I'll keep this in that key.

My family is from Champlain. Four Hubbell brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother, Frances, wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed up by a relative in the late 1940s, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published the whole correspondence as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

It might make good radio: the letters read aloud, a present-day descendant, and a few things the public war record knew about these men that the family never did. Happy to record an interview or hand Emily Russell the Plattsburgh-side material — whatever works for your newsroom.
