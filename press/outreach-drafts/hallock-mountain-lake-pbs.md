**Subject:** A Champlain family's Civil War letters — a Mountain Lake Journal segment

Hi Thom,

I read your reflections on covering the North Country, and the throughline — that the region's history lives in its families — is what I want to bring you.

My family is from Champlain. Four Hubbell brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother, Frances, wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed up by a relative in the late 1940s, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published the whole correspondence as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

It's unusually visual for a letters story: the original pages, an animated map of where the brothers were, a timeline you can scrub. With a living descendant and a few discoveries the public record held that the family never knew, it could make a good Journal segment or roundtable topic. Glad to show you around it.
