Dear Ms. Johnson-Roehr,

JSTOR Daily does the thing I spent this past year doing at a household scale — putting a general reader next to the primary record — so I wanted to bring you the essay version.

My family kept 273 Civil War letters: four Hubbell brothers and their mother, Frances, of Champlain, NY, 1861–1870. I transcribed and structured the whole correspondence and cross-referenced it against digitized historical newspapers, including the Library of Congress's Chronicling America. The newspapers corroborated, dated, and occasionally corrected what the letters claimed. I used AI throughout, but as a strict filter — never a source — and identified every person by hand.
