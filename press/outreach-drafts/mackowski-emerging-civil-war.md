Dr. Mackowski — ECW has always made room for the new voice with a real story to tell, so I wanted to bring you mine.

I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of Alexander F. Hubbell, 60th New York, wounded at Lookout Mountain and through the Atlanta Campaign. He was one of four brothers from Champlain, in far-northern New York. His brother Henry, of the 34th New York, was first reported missing at Antietam, then confirmed killed. Charles and James served in the 153rd New York. Their mother Frances wrote through all of it. The family left 273 letters from 1861 to 1870, which I recovered, transcribed, and cross-referenced against pension and regimental records — using AI strictly as a judge over real documents, never as a source.
