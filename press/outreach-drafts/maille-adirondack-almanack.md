**Subject:** A guest piece: 273 Civil War letters from a Champlain family

Hi Ezra,

I read the Almanack for its history writing — the Clinton County and North Country pieces especially — so I wanted to bring you one from my own family.

The Hubbells were a Champlain family. Four brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother, Frances, wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived; my great-grandmother-in-law Gladys typed them out in the late 1940s, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published the whole correspondence as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

I'd like to write a short piece for the Almanack — the family story first, and a plain account of the method (modern tools used carefully, never to invent anything). Or if you'd rather, a brief news note that the archive is now public. Whatever fits your pages.
