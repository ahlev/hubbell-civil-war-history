**Subject:** 273 Civil War letters from a Champlain family, finally readable

Hi Robin,

Your piece on the Timbuctoo marker stuck with me — you cover the North Country stories that almost slipped the record. I have one from your county.

My family is from Champlain. Four Hubbell brothers went to war in 1861, and they and their mother Frances wrote home — 273 letters across four theaters, from Virginia to the Atlanta Campaign. The letters sat in a trunk, then my great-grandmother-in-law Gladys typed them out in the late 1940s, and they more or less waited. I'm Alexander F. Hubbell's great-great-grandson and namesake, and over the past year I transcribed, organized, and published the whole correspondence as an interactive archive — and along the way the public record told us things about these brothers their own family never knew.
