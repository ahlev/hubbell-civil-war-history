**Subject:** An old box of family letters, recovered with AI — without letting it make anything up

Andy — your investigations into AI training data and provenance are part of why I built this the way I did. I'm a tech researcher in Athens, GA, and the namesake of a Civil War soldier; over the past while I recovered, transcribed, and structured 273 of my family's letters (1861–1870) into a queryable dataset and nine interactive views.

The discipline I'm proudest of is the one you'd care about: AI did the transcription and cross-checking grunt work, but it's kept out of the source-of-truth layer entirely — every computed inference is separated from the original text, and people are matched by curated alias tables, never fuzzy guessing. It's a small, receipts-first counter-example to the slop.

It feels like a Waxy kind of project: one person, an odd personal archive, built with care. Want a quick walkthrough, or the dataset to poke at?
