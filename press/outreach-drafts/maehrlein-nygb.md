Hi Lauren,

I've been following NYG&B's work on a guide to New York's state archives, and it nudged me to write — because my project is a New York research story I'd love to offer *The New York Researcher*.

The Hubbells were a Champlain family in Clinton County. Four brothers enlisted in 1861; they and their mother Frances wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed by a relative in the late 1940s, digitized by my father, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published them as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers; the surviving line settled in Iowa.

I'd write it New York-first: the family, the letters, and what New York and federal records added — then a candid note on method, since identity was resolved by curated alias tables rather than fuzzy matching, with AI used only as a disciplined judge. Mostly: what other New York families could do.
