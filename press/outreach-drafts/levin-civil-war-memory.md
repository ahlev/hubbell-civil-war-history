**Subject:** Five generations of remembering one family's war

Kevin — your conversation posts and your insistence on close, honest reading of primary sources are why I read Civil War Memory, and why I thought of you for this.

I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of Alexander F. Hubbell, 60th New York, wounded at Lookout Mountain and through the Atlanta Campaign. His brother Henry, of the 34th New York, was reported missing at Antietam and then confirmed killed. The family left 273 letters, 1861 to 1870. But the memory thread is what might interest you most: my great-grandmother Gladys typed the letters in 1947–49, my father Bruce digitized them, and I published them in 2026. Three acts of remembering across five generations.
