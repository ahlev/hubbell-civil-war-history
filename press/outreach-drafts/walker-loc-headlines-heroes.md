**Subject:** A worked example: a family's letters, verified against Chronicling America

Dear Ms. Walker,

Your Headlines & Heroes posts on navigating Chronicling America were a practical guide for me this past year, so I wanted to show you where they led.

My family kept 273 Civil War letters: four Hubbell brothers and their mother, Frances, of Champlain, NY, 1861–1870. I transcribed and structured the whole correspondence, then used Chronicling America to date, corroborate, and in a few places correct what the letters said — battles, movements, and homefront news. I used AI throughout, but only as a filter, never a source, and identified every person by hand.

It struck me that this is a concrete payoff of exactly what your blog teaches — private papers meeting the digitized public record. If it would be useful to readers, I'd gladly write up the method as a worked example, or just share it with the division. A look first: [dashboard link]
