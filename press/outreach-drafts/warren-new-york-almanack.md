**Subject:** Submission: a Champlain family's 273 Civil War letters

Hi John,

The Almanack is where I go to read New York's history taken seriously, sourcing and all — so I'd like to offer it a piece from my own family.

The Hubbells were a Champlain family. Four brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother, Frances, wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed up by a family member in the late 1940s, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published the entire correspondence as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

I'd write it history-first: the family and the letters, then a candid note on method — the public record was the source, and the tools only organized and checked it, never invented. There are a few things the archives knew about these men that the family never did.
