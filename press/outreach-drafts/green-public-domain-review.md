**Subject:** A recovered 1860s family correspondence, cross-referenced against the newspaper archive

Dear Adam,

The Public Domain Review is where I go to watch old primary sources come back to life, so I'd like to offer it one of my own family's.

The Hubbells of Champlain, NY left 273 letters — four brothers who enlisted in 1861 and their mother, Frances, writing across the Civil War and after. This year I transcribed, structured, and published the full correspondence and cross-referenced it against digitized historical newspapers, including the Library of Congress's Chronicling America. The work used AI throughout, but strictly as a filter — never a source — with every person identified by hand.

There are wonderfully strange, human threads in here, and a larger idea I'd love to write toward: that every family archive structured this way enlarges the shared public record. I could write a single-thread essay or a piece on the method. One link to see it: [dashboard link]
