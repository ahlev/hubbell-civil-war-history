**Subject:** "You can do this": recovering 273 family letters

Hi Amy,

Your line that family history should be enjoyable — genealogy without the frustration — is basically the moral of my own project, so I wanted to share it.

I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of a Civil War soldier. The Hubbells were a Champlain, New York family; four brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother Frances wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed by a relative in 1947–49, digitized by my father, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published them as an interactive archive. The surviving line settled in Iowa.

What I think your audience would take from it: an amateur really can recover and organize a family correspondence, with steps anyone could follow — and a simple rule that keeps it honest (let the record be the source; the tools only organize and check it).
