**Subject:** A Champlain family's 273 Civil War letters, now an archive

Hi Carly,

You spend your days on Clinton County as it is now. I've spent the last year on Clinton County as it was — and I think there's a local story in it.

My family is from Champlain. Four Hubbell brothers went off to the Civil War in 1861; they and their mother, Frances, wrote home across four theaters of fighting. 273 letters survived, typed up by a family member in the late 1940s, and I finally transcribed, organized, and published the whole correspondence as an interactive archive this year. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

What makes it a story isn't the software — it's that the public war record held details about these men their own family never knew, and now you can read the letters alongside the facts. Happy to give you a guided look or an exclusive on a discovery.
