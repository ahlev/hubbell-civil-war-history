**Subject:** A family's real-time search for a soldier "missing" at Antietam

Frank — Shrouded Veterans is about recovering the men who slipped out of the record, so I wanted to share a primary source that's all about that fear in real time.

I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of Alexander F. Hubbell, 60th New York. His brother Henry, of the 34th New York, was first reported missing at Antietam, then confirmed killed. The family's letters — 273 of them, 1861 to 1870, from Champlain, New York — trace the whole arc: the not-knowing, the rumors, and finally the confirmation. It reads like the human side of the records you work from to find and mark these men.

I transcribed and cross-referenced the corpus against pension and regimental records, using AI only as a check against real documents. If Henry's story, or the broader "missing soldier" thread in the letters, is ever useful for Shrouded Veterans or an ECW piece, I'd gladly share everything I have on him.
