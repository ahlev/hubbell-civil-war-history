**Subject:** A primary-source corpus from your kind of under-covered ground

Drew — your blog has always given the small-press and local-history primary sources the attention the trade houses skip, so this felt like one to put in front of you.

I'm an amateur historian and the great-great-grandson of Alexander F. Hubbell, 60th New York, wounded at Lookout Mountain and through the Atlanta Campaign. He and his three brothers, from Champlain in far-northern New York, left 273 family letters from 1861 to 1870 — including the stretch when their brother Henry of the 34th New York was reported missing at Antietam, then confirmed killed. It crosses exactly the ground you cover most: North Country home front plus the Western theater.

I transcribed and structured the whole corpus and cross-referenced it against pension and regimental records, using AI only as a check against real documents. If a sourced primary-archive project is ever worth a note or a look for your readers, I'd be glad to walk you through it.
