**Subject:** Clinton County to Clinton County: a Civil War letter archive for your column

Hi Lawrence,

I've read your Almanack history columns for a while, and your work on Clinton County is part of why I kept going on a project of my own.

My family — the Hubbells — were a Champlain family. Four brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother, Frances, wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed up by a relative in the late 1940s, and over this past year I transcribed, structured, and published the full correspondence as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

You'd see things in it I can't, and I'd value that. There may be a column in it: a Clinton County family's war, in their own words, with a few details the public record added that the family never had. I'd be glad to give you first look and sit for an interview.
