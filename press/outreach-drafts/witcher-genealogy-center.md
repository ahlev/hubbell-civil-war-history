Hi Curt,

"Everyone has a story" and "the power of story" are the phrases you've built a career around — and they're the reason I'm writing.

The Hubbells were a Champlain, New York family. Four brothers enlisted in 1861; they and their mother Frances wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed by a relative in 1947–49, digitized by my father, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published them as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers, and the surviving line settled in Iowa.

I'd love to offer a short piece for *Genealogy Gems*, or a talk for the Center: how an amateur recovered and verified a family correspondence, with a method other families and small repositories could copy — identity resolved by curated alias tables, never fuzzy matching, and AI used only as a disciplined judge, a filter never a source.
