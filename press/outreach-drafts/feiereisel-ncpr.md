**Subject:** North Country at war and at work — 273 letters from one family

Hi Amy,

"Making small things monumental" is how you describe your work, and it's the best short description of what I've spent a year on.

My family is from Champlain. Four Hubbell brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother, Frances, wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived — full of the un-monumental stuff: wages, debts, a brother's illness, who was getting the hay in. I transcribed, structured, and published the whole correspondence as an interactive archive this past year. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

It feels close to North Country at Work — ordinary people's own record of their working lives, just 160 years back. I'd love to show you the letters about money and labor and survival, and I'm happy to sit for an interview.
