**Subject:** Charts that try not to lie — built on a deliberately honest dataset

Alberto — *How Charts Lie* changed how I thought about this project, so I wanted to bring it to you. I'm an amateur historian who recovered and structured 273 of my family's Civil War letters (1861–1870), then built nine interactive visualizations on top of them: a timeline, an animated map, a social network, a health ledger, a money story.

The thing I tried hardest to get right is the part you teach: the data has to be honest before the chart can be. So every letter is split into fact, editorial judgment, and computed analysis as separate layers, inferences carry a three-axis confidence score, and AI is used only as a filter over real records, never as a source. The visualizations only ever show what the data can actually support.

I'd value your eye on it — as a case study, or just a conversation. Happy to share the visuals and the underlying schema.
