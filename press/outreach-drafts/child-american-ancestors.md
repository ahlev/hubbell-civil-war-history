Hi Christopher,

Your work with Meaghan Siekman on *Family Tales and Trials* — reconstructing families from fragmentary record — is close to the labor I want to write about.

The Hubbells were a Champlain, New York family. Four brothers enlisted in 1861; they and their mother Frances wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed by a relative in the late 1940s, digitized by my father, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published them as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers; the line settled in Iowa.

The part I'd want to put before a sourcing-minded reader: every person mention is resolved by curated alias tables, never fuzzy matching, and the AI worked only as a disciplined judge — a filter, never a source. The public record was the source; the tools organized and checked it.
