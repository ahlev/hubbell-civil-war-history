**Subject:** Ancestors' stories told: a New York family's 273 Civil War letters

Hi Jane,

"Your ancestors want their stories to be told" is the tagline of your show — and it's almost exactly what my project set out to do, so I had to write.

The Hubbells were a Champlain family in Clinton County, New York. Four brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother Frances wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed by a relative in 1947–49, digitized by my father, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published them as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers; the surviving line settled in Iowa.

I'd love to come on the Forget-Me-Not Hour to talk about it — the family and the letters, the five-generation chain of custody, and what New York records added. I kept it honest with curated alias tables instead of fuzzy guesses, letting the record be the source.
