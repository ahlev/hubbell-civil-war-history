**Subject:** A Plattsburgh-area family's Civil War letters, read in their own voices

Hi Emily,

You're the one at NCPR covering Plattsburgh and the Adirondacks, so this is for your beat.

My family is from Champlain. Four Hubbell brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother, Frances, wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed up by a relative in the late 1940s, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published the entire correspondence as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

I think it could be a strong feature — the letters carry well read aloud, and there's a living-descendant thread plus a few things the public record knew about these men that the family didn't. I'd be glad to record an interview or just walk you through it first.
