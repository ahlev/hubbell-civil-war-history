**Subject:** Four brothers' voices from 1861, recovered and searchable

Hi Hunter,

Your essay on Mary MacLane — that hungry, unguarded young voice — is the register I keep hearing in my own family's papers, so I wanted to bring them to you.

The Hubbells of Champlain, NY left 273 Civil War letters: four brothers who enlisted in 1861 and their mother, Frances, writing across the war and after. This year I transcribed, structured, and published the whole correspondence and cross-referenced it against digitized newspapers, including the Library of Congress's Chronicling America. I used AI throughout, but only as a filter — never a source — and identified every person by hand.

What I'd love to write for PDR is a single-thread essay built around one of these voices, set against the public record of the same months — with a quiet larger point that every family archive done this way grows the shared one. A look first: [dashboard link]
