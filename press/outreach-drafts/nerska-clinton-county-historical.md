**Subject:** A Champlain family's Civil War letters — for the CCHA's county

Dear Ms. Nerska,

I came across the history of the CCHA and was glad to see how carefully Clinton County keeps its own record. I have something from that record I'd like to share with you.

My family — the Hubbells — were a Champlain family. Four brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother, Frances, wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed up by a relative in the late 1940s, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published the entire correspondence as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

It's Clinton County history, and I'd like it to be useful to you — whether as a resource for the collection, a members' talk, or a newsletter note. I'd welcome your eyes on it and any names or places you recognize.
