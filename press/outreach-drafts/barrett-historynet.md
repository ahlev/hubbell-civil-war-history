**Subject:** How a family recovered 273 Civil War letters with AI — without inventing any history

Claire,

Most "AI meets history" stories make me wince, so here's one built the opposite way. My family held 273 letters that four Hubbell brothers and their mother wrote home to Champlain, New York, from 1861 to 1870. I used AI strictly as a checker against muster and pension records — *filter, never a source* — so it never invents a record, only flags and verifies.

That discipline surfaced real things: Henry Hubbell of the 34th New York was reported *missing* at Antietam and only later confirmed *killed*, body never recovered; one captain, Davis J. Rich, turns out to have commanded two of the brothers in two different regiments; and my great-great-grandfather's Lookout Mountain wound checked out against independent records.
