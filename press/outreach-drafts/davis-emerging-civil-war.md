Mr. Davis — *A Long and Bloody Task* traces Dalton through Kennesaw to the Chattahoochee, which happens to be the exact route my ancestor marched, so I wanted to bring this to you. I live in Athens and I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of Alexander F. Hubbell, a 60th New York soldier in that campaign (wounded earlier at Lookout Mountain). Using AI, I recovered, transcribed, and structured 273 of his family's letters from 1861 to 1870 into a searchable archive, then cross-referenced them against public pension and regimental records.

It reads like a foot-soldier companion to the narrative you wrote — the same ground, from inside the column. If it's worth an Emerging Civil War look, or just your own read, I'd be glad to open up the full archive and point you to the campaign letters.
