**Subject:** One Champlain family's Civil War, told in 273 letters

Hi Melissa,

You've reported across the Champlain Valley — Burlington and the North Country both — so you know how local this story is before I even tell it.

My family is from Champlain. Four Hubbell brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother, Frances, wrote home through four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived in the family, were typed up in the late 1940s, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published the whole correspondence as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

There's a regional-history feature here — what one family's nine years of letters reveal about the North Country at war — and the archive works as a lasting community resource. I built it carefully, with the rule that the tools could organize and check the record but never invent it. Glad to give you or Ezra a full look, or an exclusive on a discovery.
