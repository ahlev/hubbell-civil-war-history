Reading about your years inside Ezra Carman's manuscript, I kept thinking that you, more than anyone, understand what it takes to make 1862's records speak clearly. I've done a much smaller version of that for one family.

My great-great-grandfather's brother, Henry Hubbell of the 34th New York, was killed at Antietam on September 17, 1862, and his body was never recovered. The public record first listed him *missing*, then confirmed *killed*. I found the thread by recovering 273 Hubbell family letters from 1861–1870 — I'm an amateur historian who used modern AI to transcribe and structure them, then cross-referenced the soldiers against the archives.
