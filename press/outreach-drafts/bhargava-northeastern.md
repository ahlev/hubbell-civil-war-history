**Subject:** Making 273 family letters legible to non-experts — a data-storytelling case

Rahul — your Data Therapy work and DataBasic are the spirit I tried to build in, so I wanted to share this with you. I'm an amateur historian who recovered and structured 273 of my family's Civil War letters (1861–1870) into a dataset, then built nine interactive views meant for ordinary readers: a timeline, an animated map, a social network, a health ledger, a money story.

The goal was legibility with integrity. Underneath, the data is a three-layer architecture — fact, editorial judgment, computed analysis — with curated person and place registries, so a general reader can explore a war through one family without the presentation outrunning the evidence. AI helped as a disciplined filter over real records, never as a source.

It feels like a teaching case in accessible, honest data storytelling — maybe even a workshop example. I'd love your read on it, and I'm happy to share the dataset and visuals.
