**Subject:** A Clinton County Civil War family, recovered — for the podcast or the journal?

Hi Devin,

As a SUNY Plattsburgh history grad, you know Clinton County ground — which is exactly where my family's story begins.

The Hubbells were a Champlain family. Four brothers enlisted in 1861, and they and their mother Frances wrote home across four theaters of the war. 273 letters survived, were typed by a relative in 1947–49, digitized by my father, and this past year I transcribed, structured, and published them as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers; the surviving line settled in Iowa.

I think it could make a good *A New York Minute in History* segment, or a note for *New York History* — the family and the letters, the five-generation chain of custody, and what New York and federal records added. I kept it honest: identity resolved by curated alias tables, never fuzzy matching, with the record as the source and the tools only organizing and checking it. And a thread on what other New York families and local historians could do.
