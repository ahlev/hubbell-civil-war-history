**Subject:** A small "shadow" in the Antietam record — missing, then killed

Mr. Frye,

*Antietam Shadows* made me look harder at the gaps and errors in the 1862 record, so I wanted to bring you a small one I found in my own family.

My great-great-grandfather's brother, Henry Hubbell of the 34th New York, was killed at Antietam on September 17, 1862, and his body was never recovered. The public record first listed him *missing*, then confirmed *killed*. I found that correction while recovering 273 Hubbell family letters from 1861–1870 — I'm an amateur historian, and I used modern AI to transcribe and structure them, then cross-referenced the soldiers against the archives.

It's a quiet example of the kind of myth-versus-record work you do at scale. If the story or the primary sources are useful to you, SHAF, or an audience you speak to, I'd be glad to share everything. The dashboard keeps the letters and the record side by side: [dashboard link].
