Meg — *The Aftermath of Battle* is the book I kept thinking about while reading my own family's letters, so I wanted to write to you directly.

I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of Alexander F. Hubbell, 60th New York. His brother Henry, of the 34th New York, was reported missing at Antietam and then, after weeks of dread, confirmed killed. The family's letters — 273 of them, 1861 to 1870, from Champlain, New York — hold that whole arc: the not-knowing, the conflicting reports, the grief settling in at home with their mother Frances. It's your subject, the burial and the bereaved, seen from the kitchen table rather than the field.
