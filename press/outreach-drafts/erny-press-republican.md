**Subject:** A Champlain family's Civil War letters — a hometown story for the P-R

Hi Melissa,

I'm writing with a North Country story that belongs in your paper more than anywhere else.

My family is from Champlain. In 1861 four Hubbell brothers enlisted, and over the next nine years they and their mother, Frances, wrote home — 273 letters, four theaters of the war. The letters survived in the family, were typed up by my great-grandmother-in-law Gladys in the late 1940s, and this past year I finished transcribing, organizing, and publishing the whole set as an interactive archive. I'm the great-great-grandson and namesake of one of the brothers.

There's a real local feature here: a Clinton County family, five generations of careful keeping, and a few things the public record knew about these men that the family never did. Happy to give Robin Caudell or whoever you'd assign full access, or an exclusive on one discovery.
