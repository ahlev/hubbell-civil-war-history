# Unique-Angle Niches (letters-culture, women's/home-front, religious, postal, typewriter) — Vetted Target List
**Verified as of:** 2026-07-07 · RESEARCH ONLY

**Top-line verdict:** the four niches are NOT equally alive. Letters-culture + women's history produced genuine gems; Presbyterian history produced one slow-but-perfect institutional fit; postal/ephemera is largely marginal (their appetite is physical covers/postmarks). **5 strong finalists + 3 conditional; diminishing returns begin sharply after #5.**

## FINALISTS (ranked)

### 1. Letters of Note — Shaun Usher (Substack) — letters-culture
The dominant letters-as-culture platform (2009 site → bestselling books → Substack, publishing weekly through **Jul 3, 2026**). ~20% of featured letters are **submitted by the public** ([About](https://lettersofnote.com/about/)); his standing submissions thread asks readers to "*paste a couple of lines or just point me to the right book or website or source.*"
- **Bridge:** don't pitch the archive — pitch **one letter**: Frances reading casualty lists "day after day... with trembling," written to a son already dead at Antietam. A letter whose central fact the writer didn't know is exactly a Letters of Note letter.
- **Ask:** 3-paragraph suggestion of the single Frances letter — transcription + provenance + rights clarity ("family-owned, permission granted" up front).
- **Contact (verified):** **shaun@lettersofnote.com** (published by Usher in his [submissions thread](https://news.lettersofnote.com/p/what-are-your-favourite-letters)).
- **Risk:** high inbound; no guarantee — but a feature here is the biggest audience in this vertical.

### 2. Nursing Clio — women's history / home-front
Long-running peer-edited blog on gender/medicine/history; *The Nursing Clio Reader* (Rutgers UP). **Active:** Jun 10 + Jun 3, 2026 posts, incl. a letters-as-source piece. Dedicated **Grief topic page**; comparables on-site: "Maternal Grief in Black and White" (2022), "'Shock from Loss'" (2018).
- **Bridge:** Frances's war — grief, coping, gendered labor — **plus the measured finding** (24% vs 2%): "emotional protection, quantified" is precisely their "the personal is historical" register.
- **Ask:** 200–500-word **pitch** (not a draft) for a 500–1,500-word essay via their form.
- **Contact (verified):** pitch form + nursingclio@gmail.com ([write-for-us](https://nursingclio.org/write-for-us/)).
- **Risk:** weeks-long editorial process; accessible tone required; the site is the source base, not the subject. *(Cross-ref: Sarah Handley-Cousins is Nursing Clio's exec editor AND a finalist in 08-shenandoah-medicine.md — one coordinated approach, not two.)*

### 3. Waymarks (Presbyterian Historical Society, formerly Journal of Presbyterian History) — religious history
PHS (est. 1852) relaunched its 100-year-old journal as *Waymarks*, explicitly **public-facing**, soliciting **"think pieces and commentaries (1,000–2,000 words)... that explicitly connect Presbyterian and Reformed history to the present"** ([writing guidelines](https://pcusa.org/historical-society/history-online/publications/journal-history/writing-journal)). PHS maintains a standing Civil War exhibit.
- **Bridge:** bullseye and unduplicated — four Presbyterian soldier-brothers whose memorial was raised by the **First Presbyterian Church Sabbath School**; letters saturated with Reformed faith language; present-day hook = the five-generation chain ending in AI transcription.
- **Ask:** 1,000–2,000-word think-piece submission (Chicago, endnotes).
- **Contact:** submission email on the writing page (Cloudflare-protected — read in a browser); PHS, 425 Lombard St, Philadelphia.
- **Risk:** several-month review; **copyright transfers to PHS on acceptance — check against reuse plans**; small circulation (credibility play).

### 4. Richard Polt — The Typewriter Revolution — typewriter/material culture
The hub of the typosphere since 1995 (Xavier philosophy professor; *The Typewriter Revolution*). **Active:** several posts weekly (Jul 6, Jun 30, Jun 27, Jun 25, 2026). Community sensibility: *"Small gestures can have large meanings. Fragile messages can be retyped on other continents."*
- **Bridge:** Gladys typing all 273 letters, 1947–49 — a typewriter as the reason a family archive survived. The typosphere adores exactly this.
- **Ask:** short personal email with the Gladys story + one 1947 typescript page scan, offered freely.
- **Contact (verified):** richard.polt@typewriterrevolution.com (on his blog).
- **Risk:** **significant framing risk** — his banner reads "resist the artificial // 100% human made." Lead with Gladys; the AI coda framed as archival continuation or omitted from the opening.

### 5. What'sHerName podcast — Dr. Katie Nelson & Olivia Meikle — women's history
Established biweekly women's-history podcast (professor sisters). **Active:** Jun 29, Jun 14, Jun 1, 2026 episodes. Format: one forgotten woman per episode + guest expert.
- **Bridge:** **Frances as an episode subject** — a mother whose war is documented in her own hand across 9 years.
- **Ask:** two-in-one email — suggest Frances as a subject AND offer the archive-builder as guest expert (their contact page explicitly invites both).
- **Contact (verified):** info[at]whatshernamepodcast.com ([contact page](https://whatshernamepodcast.com/contact-us/)) — **email, not the form** (form silently dropped submissions in late 2025).
- **Risk:** months-long backlog; guests are often authors — the archive is the credential.

## CONDITIONAL / SECOND-TIER
### 6. Unsung History — Kelly Therese Pollock (podcast)
Active biweekly (Jun 29, 2026); narrative + expert interview; proven soldiers'-families territory ("Black Soldiers & their Families in the Civil War," 2023). **Catch:** guests skew credentialed academics; suggest the *topic* and offer self or a partnering scholar. One email, medium probability.
### 7. The Civil War Philatelist — Civil War Philatelic Society — postal
Real pipeline: quarterly peer-reviewed journal, Article Coordinator **Randy Smith, rpsmith2022@gmail.com** ([submission how-to](https://www.civilwarphilatelicsociety.org/civil-war-philatelist-cp/how-to-submit-an-article/)); 2026 convention Aug 28–30. **Catch — GATE ON HOLDINGS:** their heart is covers/postmarks/routes. **If the family holds original covers/envelopes, this becomes top-5** (one family's mail circuit across four theaters + the information-lag viz = a genuinely novel article). Typescripts-only = marginal. **Ask the user what physical mail artifacts survive before pitching.**
### 8. John Fea — The Way of Improvement Leads Home — religious history (high-risk/high-reward)
**Current (currentpub.com) is DEAD — ceased Apr 4, 2025.** Fea's personal blog is intensely active (three posts Jul 7, 2026); jfea@messiah.edu on-site; Media Requests page. American religious history + loud skepticism of shoddy AI history. **He may critique rather than feature** — pitch only with the methodology paper attached, framed as "an AI-history project built the way a historian would demand."

## CONSIDERED AND REJECTED
- **Current (currentpub.com)** — ceased publication Apr 4, 2025.
- **Anxious Bench (Patheos)** — active but closed contributor roster; no intake.
- **Christian History magazine** — single-theme issues, hand-selected experts; odds too low.
- **National Women's History Museum blog** — in-house content only.
- **Smithsonian NPM "Pushing the Envelope"** — active but staff/curator-written. **Relationship note: curator Lynn Heidelbaugh co-edited *Between Home and the Front: Civil War Letters of the Walters Family* (2022) — the single most relevant curator in America for this archive; a courtesy "for your awareness" note is a worthwhile long-game move.**
- **La Posta** — semi-active, no visible intake; CWPS covers the ground with a real pipeline.
- **Ephemera Society of America** — *printed* ephemera scope; manuscript letters off-mission.
- **The Letter Exchange** — dormant/no verifiable activity.

## DIMINISHING-RETURNS VERDICT
Five gems worth real effort (each wants a *different* asset — no cannibalization), three conditionals, then closed rosters, dead publications, and artifact mismatch. **Cap unique-angle outreach at 5 (+3 conditional) and spend the remaining budget on mainstream verticals.**
