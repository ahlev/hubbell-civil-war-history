# Education, Institutions & Community — Vetted Outreach Targets
**Project:** The Hubbell Civil War Letters · **Vertical positioning:** free primary-source teaching & research resource — complete two-way family correspondence, searchable and visualized
**Research date:** 2026-07-07 · All contact channels verified against live pages; nothing invented.

## FINALISTS (ranked)

### 1. New York State Military Museum & Veterans Research Center — unit-file listing (HIGHEST VALUE, DO FIRST)
- **Institution:** NYS Military Museum, Saratoga Springs NY (NY Division of Military & Naval Affairs).
- **Evidence of fit (read directly):**
  - The [60th Infantry unit page](https://museum.dmna.ny.gov/unit-history/infantry-1/60th-infantry-regiment) carries an **"Other Resources" section that explicitly solicits additions**: "This is meant to be a comprehensive list. If, however, you know of a resource that is not listed below, please send an email."
  - Direct precedent for family letters inside a unit file: the [Hill Family Letter Collection under the 153rd NY page](https://museum.dmna.ny.gov/unit-history/infantry-2/153rd-infantry-regiment/hill-family-letter-collection).
  - All three regiments have maintained files: [34th NY](https://museum.dmna.ny.gov/unit-history/infantry/34th-infantry-regiment/historical-sketch), [60th NY](https://museum.dmna.ny.gov/unit-history/infantry-1/60th-infantry-regiment), [153rd NY](https://museum.dmna.ny.gov/unit-history/infantry-2/153rd-infantry-regiment).
- **The bridge:** one submission → three permanent listings (34th, 60th, 153rd NY) — a permanent, official state-government citation every future researcher of these regiments will find. **The single most compounding placement available to this project.** Second-stage: offer transcription files as a contributed digital collection (Hill Family precedent).
- **Contact (verified):** the on-page email invitation + `ng.ny.nyarng.list.historians@army.mil` ([contact page](https://museum.dmna.ny.gov/contact)).
- **Risk flags:** state-agency mailbox — slow; periodic closure/relocation news cycles. Low effort — send early and let it ride.

### 2. New York Almanack — article submission (FASTEST, CLEAREST INTAKE)
- **Person:** John Warren, founder/editor, `nyalmanack@gmail.com`.
- **Evidence of fit:** [Submission guidelines](https://www.newyorkalmanack.com/about-page/new-york-history-blog-article-submission-guidelines/): 750–1,600 words, Word attachment, 1–3 images (≥820×360px) with sourced captions, 3–4 sentence author bio, "prefers articles well-grounded in primary sources." They publish exactly this genre: ["Dear Friend: Letters From A Union Soldier"](https://www.newyorkalmanack.com/2023/12/dear-friend-letters-from-union-soldier/) (Dec 2023); ["Lion Courage: A New Paltz Soldier During The Civil War"](https://www.newyorkalmanack.com/2020/06/lion-courage-a-new-paltz-solider-during-the-civil-war/).
- **The bridge:** Champlain is core territory; readership is local historians, society volunteers, municipal historians — the audience that produces secondary adoption. Pitch: "Four Brothers from Champlain," July 4, 1863 Gettysburg-field letter as the hook.
- **The ask:** write the piece and submit it complete, per guidelines (author-written, not a pitch).
- **Risk flags:** one-person operation; no response SLA. Mitigate with a guideline-conformant package.

### 3. Clinton County Historical Association (Plattsburgh) — home-county anchor + lecture slot
- **Contact (verified):** `info@clintoncountyhistorical.org`, (518) 561-0340 ([site](https://www.clintoncountyhistorical.org/)).
- **Evidence of fit:** **Monday Night Lecture Series** (free, first Monday, April–December); published a 606-page two-volume **Civil War Record** naming 5,600+ Clinton County enlistees; maintains a [Civil War Records feature page](https://www.clintoncountyhistorical.org/civil-war-records) publishing individual soldier stories.
- **The bridge:** the Hubbells are Clinton County men — a complete 273-letter family archive for county soldiers is the biggest primary-source event this society will see this decade.
- **Adoption:** (a) Monday Night Lecture; (b) permanent cross-reference with their Civil War Record; (c) **the on-ramp to New York Heritage/DPLA** — CCHA can sponsor the collection into [New York Heritage](https://nyheritage.org/about) via the [Northern New York Library Network](https://nnyln.org/) (free Digitization Service Center; NY Heritage already curates a [Civil War Letters collection](https://nyheritage.org/collections/civil-war-letters) and feeds DPLA — DPLA only accepts content through hubs).
- **Risk flags:** volunteer-supported, Wed–Sat hours; lecture calendar books ahead; NY Heritage ingestion needs OAI-compatible metadata — a real project. (Parallel quick win: deposit transcriptions + PDF at the **Internet Archive** for a permanent citable copy — no gatekeeper.)

- **Contacts (verified via [Georgia Battlefields Association directory](https://georgiabattlefields.org/round_tables.aspx)):**
  - **Northeast Georgia CWRT** — second Sunday, 3 p.m., Oconee County Library, Watkinsville GA (≈10 miles from Athens — the creator's home roundtable). Contact: Steve Narrie, 770-972-0528, `negacwrt@yahoo.com`.
  - **Atlanta CWRT** — monthly dinner meetings, "top experts"; contact Tim Whalen (2025-26 president per [atlantacwrt.org](https://atlantacwrt.org/)), 770-584-3586, `whalen315@aol.com`. Cobb County CWRT (`cobbcivilwarrt@gmail.com`) is a same-metro second slot.
  - **CWRT Congress** — ["5-Star Speaker Registry"](https://www.cwrtcongress.org/speaker-registry.php); listing appears to come via roundtable recommendation — it's the *result* of the first talks, not the entry point.
- **The bridge:** ready-made talk — "Four Brothers, Four Theaters — and What the AI Had to Prove Before We Believed It." The 60th NY marched the **Atlanta Campaign** (Peach Tree Creek, Atlanta — [NYSMM unit history](https://museum.dmna.ny.gov/unit-history/infantry-1/60th-infantry-regiment)) — a Georgia audience hears about letters written on ground they know.
- **Risk flags:** volunteer-run; directory contact data goes stale (atlantacwrt.org TLS cert currently expired; venue listings conflict — confirm before writing); NE Georgia CWRT older listings show a prior Gwinnett location — verify by phone/email; Atlanta books months out.

### 5. H-Net — H-CivWar / H-Announce announcement + Resources Tab listing
- **Process (documented):** free account → "Post an Announcement" ([H-Announce](https://networks.h-net.org/h-announce?ID=221515)); for digital projects, ask a network editor to add the **"Resources Tab" category** so the project persists on the network's resources page ([H-War Resources](https://networks.h-net.org/node/12840/h-war-resources) shows the durable format; [H-CivWar](https://networks.h-net.org/h-civwar) is the target).
- **The bridge:** the methodology paper is the academic hook — H-CivWar readers assign primary-source projects and need a reason to take a Vercel-hosted family archive seriously.
- **Risk flags:** volunteer moderation, days-to-weeks; neutral scholarly register required. (See also 07-digital-humanities.md finalist #3 — richer detail incl. the DH resource page and interview series.)

### 6. American Battlefield Trust — education-team resource inquiry
- **Evidence of fit:** [Educators hub](https://www.battlefields.org/learn/educators) (free classroom materials); they already list **external** resources ([Additional Teacher and Student Civil War Curriculum Resources](https://www.battlefields.org/additional-teacher-and-student-civil-war-curriculum-resources) links to National Archives worksheets, Virginia Center for Digital History); [primary-sources teaching content](https://www.battlefields.org/learn/educators/curriculum/6-primary-sources-american-civil-war) shows soldier letters are their teaching format.
- **The bridge:** free, ad-free, fully transcribed archive spanning Antietam, Gettysburg, the Atlanta Campaign, and Cedar Creek — four battlefields the Trust actively preserves. One battlefields.org link is a high-authority durable education citation.
- **Contact:** [contact page](https://www.battlefields.org/about/contact), addressed to the Education Department. No documented intake — one well-aimed email, not a campaign. (Warm path: ECW contributor Sarah Kay Bierle now works in ABT Education — see 03-national-civil-war.md.)
- **Risk flags:** expect silence as the default; ranked below guaranteed-intake channels for that reason.

- **Emerging Civil War** — belongs to the national CW press vertical (it's there, ranked #2).
- **Cobb County / Dalton / Washington GA CWRTs** — real, but second-tier stops after NE Georgia and Atlanta prove the talk.
