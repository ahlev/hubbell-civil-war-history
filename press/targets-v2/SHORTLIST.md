## ⚠ The one fact that governs everything
**Henry Hubbell (34th NY, Co. D) was KILLED at Antietam, Sept 17, 1862** — Phase 3a `confirmed`; body never recovered; last letter July 28, 1862 (Harrison's Landing). The mother's Sept 21 letter ("I read the sad record day after day... with trembling") was written to a son already four days dead; the family's 1862–63 letters trace missing → hope → "not the least doubt that Henry was killed." Antietam on the Web already lists him as KIA (record #8050) — our material *enriches* that record, it does not correct it. Every Antietam-facing pitch uses these facts.
