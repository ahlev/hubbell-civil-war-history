> ## ⚠ FACT CORRECTION (post-scout, verified against project data 2026-07-07)
> The scout was briefed that Henry "survived" Antietam. **That briefing was wrong.** Phase 3a (`04-analysis/PHASE-3-GAP-ANALYSIS.md` §C.1) confirms: **Henry Hubbell, 34th NY Co. D, KIA Antietam Sept 17, 1862 — status `confirmed`; body never recovered** (his absence from the cemetery roll was evidence, not a gap); mother's pension file `Mo C 58119`. His last letter: LTR-1862-07-28-001, Harrison's Landing. The 1863 letters trace the family's missing→killed collapse ("There is not the least doubt that Henry was killed. We would have heard from him long before this if he had been living").
> **Consequences:** (1) The AotW record #8050 (KIA 9/17/1862) is *consistent* with our data — the "your database is wrong" angle is DEAD; do not use. (2) The Downey ask becomes pure *enrichment*: 59 Henry letters + the family's real-time missing→killed sequence attached to his existing KIA record. (3) The triptych is now a tragedy: the mother's Sept 21 letter was written to a son already four days dead — this is the strongest single story beat in the entire project. All bridges below survive this correction; the pitch copy must reflect it.

## Headline discovery (as reported by scout, read with the correction above)

**Antietam on the Web lists "Henry Hubbell, Pvt, Co. D, 34th New York — KIA 09/17/1862"** (record #8050, https://antietam.aotw.org/officers.php?officer_id=8050, sourced to the NY AG register, 1900 issue). The 34th NY roster page (https://antietam.aotw.org/officers.php?unit_id=351) shows 133 profiled individuals; casualties 32 KIA / 109 WIA / 9 MIA — 48.2% loss. The 34th drew one company from **Clinton County** (Champlain's county), per the NYS Military Museum unit history.
