- **Active:** EP41 "MyHeritage Announces Scribe AI" (Apr 6, 2026); EP40 (Mar 24, 2026) — [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-family-history-ai-show/id1749873836). - **Recent works read:** EP41 (Apr 6, 2026, guest interview format confirmed — Dave Vance of FamilyTreeDNA); EP40 (Mar 24, 2026 — Family Locket guests on RLP+AI); Thompson's "Turn Your AI into A Genealogy Research Team" (Jun 16, 2026): *"With the right prompt, any model, even a free one, can work like a small research team that checks its own work for you."* Little's site banner sensibility: AI *"can sound confident while being completely wrong"* ([aigenealogyinsights.com](https://aigenealogyinsights.com/)).
- **The bridge:** B2 dead-center — a 273-letter corpus done with curated identity tables, no fuzzy matching, human verification of every fact is exactly the disciplined-workflow case study they interview people about; B3/B4 give them the "AI-assisted research that *corrected* the record" angle they argue for constantly.
- **How they communicate:** practitioner-to-practitioner, workflow-specific, skeptical-optimist; they respond to concrete methodology with receipts, not product pitches.
- **Contact (verified):** Thompson's contact page [makingfamilyhistory.com/contact/](https://makingfamilyhistory.com/contact/); Little: AIGenealogyInsights@gmail.com (listed on [aigenealogyinsights.com](https://aigenealogyinsights.com/)); Little's Substack [vibegenealogy.ai](https://vibegenealogy.ai/) (his site moved there Jan 2026, 2,000+ subscribers).
