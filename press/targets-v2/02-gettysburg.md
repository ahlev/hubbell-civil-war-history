### 2. Dr. Charles "Charlie" Fennell — Licensed Battlefield Guide; THE Culp's Hill specialist

That is primary evidence squarely inside his dissertation's footprint — possibly names he can cross-check against muster rolls on a tour stop.
**Recent works (verified):** Addressing Gettysburg AAGG #130, Jun 25, 2026 (Apple Podcasts listing); Gettysburg Daily Culp's Hill series parts 1–8 (evergreen, https://www.gettysburgdaily.com/battlefield-guides/charlie-fennell/culps-hill/).

**Contact:** No personal email publicly verifiable. Routes: ALBG — phone (717) 337-1709, PO Box 4152, Gettysburg PA 17325, contact link at https://gettysburgtourguides.org/; or via Addressing Gettysburg (he's a recurring guest — a joint pitch to Callery covers both).
