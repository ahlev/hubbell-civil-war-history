# MASTER TARGET INDEX — The Hubbell Civil War Letters Outreach
**Built:** 2026-07-07 · 13 vetted vertical reports, ~70 finalists researched to the evidence bar, ranked here as one navigable list. **Nothing has been sent.**

## How to read this
- **Every entry links to its full dossier** (file + rank within it) — evidence, quotes from their recent work, verified contact sourcing, and risk flags live there. This index is for deciding; the reports are for writing.
- **TYPE** tells you what kind of win it is: `PRESS` (they cover you) · `POD` (guest episode) · `WRITE` (you author the piece) · `JOURNAL` (peer-reviewed/edited submission) · `INST` (durable institutional listing/programming) · `REL` (expert relationship, compounds later) · `AMP` (amplifier of other wins).
- **PRIORITY**: `A` = do in the first wave (fit + activity + open door all verified) · `B` = strong, second wave or gated on a prereq · `C` = conditional/background bet.
- **ASSET** is the ONE story each target gets (never pitch two): `TRIPTYCH` (Antietam: Henry KIA, brother searching, mother writing to a dead son) · `JULY4` (Culp's Hill field letter) · `JAMES` (the 160-year death-date correction) · `LAG` (information-lag: what each writer didn't know and when — computed from dates, fully robust) · `METHOD` (the "filter, never a source" methodology) · `CHAIN` (five-generation stewardship / Gladys's typescripts) · `FRANCES` (the mother's war) · `RESOURCE` (the archive as free teaching/research infrastructure) · `GEORGIA` (Athens namesake / Lookout Mountain / Atlanta Campaign) · `HEALTH` (health ledger) · `ENSEMBLE` (four brothers, one mail circuit).
- **`ASYM` RETIRED (2026-07-08, user call):** the quantified asymmetry-of-register finding (24% vs 2%, 17 pairs) is held back from all press-facing use — not robust enough under scrutiny to lead with. Qualitative letter contrasts (James's plain wound account to his brother vs. reassurance home) may appear *inside* other stories since they rest on verbatim documents. Targets previously anchored on ASYM are re-anchored below.

## ⚠ Standing rules
1. **Henry died at Antietam** (KIA, West Woods, body never recovered) — every pitch uses the corrected facts.
2. **One person = one pitch = one ask.** These communities interlock (Antietam Institute↔SHAF↔Banks; ECW contributors; FHAI↔Family Locket↔NGS) and compare notes.
3. **ECW gets ONE guest post first** (recommend TRIPTYCH for Sept 17), the Cedar Creek/James piece later if it lands.
4. **ASYM retired from press use** (see legend note) — no first-publication decision needed; the LAG story replaces it where a data angle is wanted.
5. **Prereqs before any send:** production URL confirmed · methodology paper live · About/Press pages live · bio final · project email created.
