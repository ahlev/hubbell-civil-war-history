## 3. Why them
Stotelmyer literally wrote the book on those who died at Antietam and South Mountain (*The Bivouacs of the Dead*). A soldier killed at Antietam whose body was never recovered — and whose record was first wrong — is precisely his subject and sensibility, and he's a Hagerstown native who guides on the ground.

## 4. Way-in (history first)
Lead: Henry Hubbell, 34th NY, killed at Antietam Sept 17, 1862, body never recovered; record first **missing**, then **killed**. The 273 Hubbell letters (1861–1870), recovered and structured with modern AI and cross-referenced to the record, document a single dead soldier and his family — directly adjacent to his *Bivouacs of the Dead* work. AI is the supporting proof point.

## 5. Personalization hook
