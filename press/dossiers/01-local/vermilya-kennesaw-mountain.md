## 3. Why them
Vermilya wrote the book on the exact battle and campaign Hubbell fought, and his Kennesaw Mountain blog already publishes **soldier letters and diary excerpts from the 1864 Georgia fighting**. A newly recovered set of 273 letters from a soldier in that campaign is, almost literally, content for his platform — and he's the ideal expert to vouch for and amplify it.
