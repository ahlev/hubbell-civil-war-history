# Dossier — Dennis E. Frye, Antietam/Harpers Ferry historian (SHAF)

**ID:** LOCAL-MD-12 · **Tier:** 1 · **Status:** drafted

## 1. Identity
- **Name:** Dennis E. Frye
- **Title:** **Chief Historian Emeritus, Harpers Ferry National Historical Park** (retired after ~32 years NPS, 20 as Chief Historian); co-founder and first president of the **Save Historic Antietam Foundation**, and current **SHAF Vice President**; co-founder and past president of the American Battlefield Trust — cited: [SHAF Board of Directors](http://shaf.org/board-of-directors/), [Shepherdstown Chronicle retirement story](https://www.shepherdstownchronicle.com/news/2018/05/11/chief-historian-retires-from-harpers-ferry-national-park/), and [American Battlefield Trust profile](https://www.battlefields.org/preserve/champions/dennis-frye). (Caution: some older bios still call him SHAF "President" — the current SHAF board page lists Tom Clemens as President, Frye as VP; verify.)
- **Beat:** Antietam, Harpers Ferry, and the 1862 Maryland Campaign; battlefield preservation and interpretation. Based in the Harpers Ferry / Shepherdstown area, adjacent to Sharpsburg.

## 2. Reach
- One of the most recognized public Civil War historians in the region: author of 11 books and 129 articles; has led tours for the Smithsonian, National Geographic, and *The New York Times* — cited: [American Battlefield Trust profile](https://www.battlefields.org/preserve/champions/dennis-frye). Specific audience figures **n/a**.

## 3. Why them
Frye is a marquee regional name with a national platform and a documented love of "provocative interpretation" that questions conventional wisdom (his *Antietam Shadows*). A well-sourced record correction and a fresh primary source are catnip; his endorsement or amplification would carry weight across the whole regional Civil War community.

## 4. Way-in (history first)
Lead: Henry Hubbell, 34th NY, killed at Antietam Sept 17, 1862, body never recovered; record first **missing**, then **killed**. The 273 Hubbell letters (1861–1870), recovered and structured with modern AI and cross-referenced to the archival record, are offered as primary source and as a "myth vs. record" story aligned with his interests. AI is the supporting proof point.

## 5. Personalization hook
His *Antietam Shadows: Mystery, Myth & Machination* "questions the conventional wisdom of the 1862 invasion" — cited: [American Battlefield Trust profile](https://www.battlefields.org/preserve/champions/dennis-frye). A casualty record that was first simply *wrong* (missing, not killed) is a small, concrete "shadow" of exactly that kind.
