**3. Why-them**
"North Country at Work" is built on exactly what this project is — ordinary North Country people's firsthand records of their lives. Her stated interest, "the way we make monumental things small and small things monumental," is almost a description of the Hubbell letters.

**4. Way-in (history-first)**
Lead with the family-and-work angle: four Champlain brothers and their mother documenting a war (and the home economy that bankrolled it) in their own hand. Frame as historical first-person testimony from working North Country people. The archive/AI is the means, not the message.

**5. Personalization hook**
Her "North Country at Work" oral-history project and her stated mission of finding the monumental in small lives line up directly with a family correspondence that records labor, money, illness, and survival across nine years. Source: https://www.npr.org/people/nx-s1-61123/amy-feiereisel
