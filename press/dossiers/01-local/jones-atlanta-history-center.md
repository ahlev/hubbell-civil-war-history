# Dossier — Gordon L. Jones, Atlanta History Center

## 1. Identity
- **Name:** Gordon L. Jones, Ph.D.
- **Employer:** Atlanta History Center — https://www.atlantahistorycenter.com/
- **Title:** Senior Military Historian and Curator (on staff since 1991). Cited: https://emergingcivilwar.com/2024/08/06/ecw-honors-gordon-jones-with-2024-ecw-award-for-service-in-civil-war-public-history/ and UGA Press author page https://www.ugapress.org/author/gordon-l-jones/

## 3. Why them
Jones is the institutional authority on the Battle of Atlanta and a master public historian — exactly the expert whose blessing or interest gives a project like this weight. He cares about objects and primary sources that make the campaign concrete; a verified run of soldier letters is a primary source he can contextualize, cite, or help surface to the AHC's audience.

## 4. Way-in (weighted)
- **Personal Georgia hook (lead):** An Athens-based namesake descendant recovered Alexander F. Hubbell's (60th NY) Atlanta Campaign letters with AI — the campaign Jones built the Cyclorama and *Turning Point* around.
- **History-first / material culture:** 273 verified letters (1861–1870), cross-referenced against public archives — a documentary counterpart to the artifacts he curates.
- **Public-history angle:** potential alignment with AHC programming/audiences; expert validation.

## 5. Personalization hook
