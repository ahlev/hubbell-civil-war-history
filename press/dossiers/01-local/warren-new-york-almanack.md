The Almanack is the front door to New York's history community. Warren both edits and publishes contributed history pieces, and he has a personal North Country/Adirondack tie (his NCPR conditions report). A Champlain-family Civil War archive is squarely on-brand and reaches exactly the readers most likely to use it.

Offer a contributed article in the creator's voice: a Champlain family's 273 Civil War letters, and what the public record added to them. Emphasize methodology integrity ("filter, never a source") because his readers care about sourcing. Secondary: a news note announcing the archive to the NYS history community.
