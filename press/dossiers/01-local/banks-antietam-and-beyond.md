## 3. Why them
Banks is a journalist who tells Antietam through individual soldiers and the ground itself — the exact register of the Hubbell story. The podcast format is ideal for a first-person "how I found my ancestor" episode, and the blog regularly profiles people doing exactly this kind of recovery work (e.g., he profiled Brian Downey of Antietam on the Web).

## 4. Way-in (history first)
Lead: Henry Hubbell, 34th NY, killed at Antietam Sept 17, 1862, body never recovered; the record first said **missing**, then **killed**. The family recovered and structured 273 letters (1861–1870) with modern AI and cross-referenced the soldiers — a clean podcast/blog narrative arc. Offer a guest segment or a guest blog post.
