Jarrett commissions exactly this kind of "useful idea + human story" essay or how-to.

**4. Way-in (archives/future-of-archives, angled to meaning)**
Lead with the human idea: what recovering a family's letters does to grief, identity, and continuity — and how, now that any family can structure and verify its own papers (here, cross-referenced against the Library of Congress's Chronicling America), this becomes a practice anyone can take up. Pair the meaning with the method's honesty (AI as filter, never source; curated identity). Offer either a Psyche "Idea" essay on memory/legacy or a how-to guide: "How to recover your family's letters."

**5. Personalization hook**
Psyche's remit — "know your self and live well" — fits a first-person account of meeting four ancestors through their own words and discovering what the public record knew that the family never did. It's self-knowledge via the archive. Source: https://psyche.co/about
