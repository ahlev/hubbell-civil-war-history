A Civil War family's 273 letters are full of exactly those voices, now structured and searchable.

**4. Way-in (archives/future-of-archives)**

Then offer what he's best at commissioning — a single-thread essay built around one vivid voice or object in the correspondence — backed by the method's rigor (AI as filter, never source; curated identity).
