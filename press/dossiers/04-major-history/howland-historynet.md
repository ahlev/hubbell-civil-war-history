## 4. Way-in (discovery + method first)
Lead with the discoveries, then the rigor:
- **Henry Hubbell, 34th New York** — first reported **missing**, later confirmed **killed** at **Antietam** (Sept 17, 1862); his body was never recovered.
- **A cross-brother officer link** — **Capt. Davis J. Rich** commanded two different Hubbell brothers' companies in two different regiments, surfaced only by cross-binding the letters to muster records.
- **A family West Point claim** verified by cross-binding independent sources.
- **An independently corroborated Lookout Mountain wound** to Alexander F. Hubbell, 60th NY.
All produced by a documented method — *"filter, never a source"* — in which modern AI acts only as a disciplined judge over real archival records and **never invents a record**. The 273 Hubbell letters (1861–1870, Champlain, NY) are the primary-source spine.
