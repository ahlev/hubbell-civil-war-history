Lapham's Quarterly is, structurally, a cross-reference engine: it sets primary documents side by side around a theme. A family archive of 273 letters — cross-referenced against the period's newspapers — is raw material in exactly that mode, and the relaunch is actively rebuilding its editorial program and looking for material/voices.
