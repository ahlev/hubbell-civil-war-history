## 3. Why them
Wagenhoffer is the field's gatekeeper for new and overlooked Civil War titles, with a documented soft spot for primary-source and local-history projects from outside the big trade houses. A self-published/independent 273-letter family archive is exactly the kind of under-the-radar primary source he flags for readers — and his coverage reaches the serious-reader audience most likely to use the dashboard.

## 4. Way-in (history-first)
- **Lead — the discovery + the family:** 273 letters (1861–1870) from the Hubbell family of Champlain, NY. Four brothers across both theaters: Henry (34th NY) killed at Antietam (first reported missing, then confirmed killed); Alexander (60th NY) wounded at Lookout Mountain and through the Atlanta Campaign; Charles and James in the 153rd NY; mother Frances at home.
- **Cross-brother officer link:** Davis J. Rich commanded two different brothers' companies in two different regiments — a documented thread across the corpus.
- **Method as proof point:** AI used strictly as a disciplined judge over real records — "filter, never a source."

## 5. Personalization hook
His About page states the blog leans toward "smaller publishers, university presses, and local history" and "all geographical areas." The hook: a primary-source corpus that spans his beloved under-covered ground — North Country New York home front plus the Western theater (Lookout Mountain, Atlanta) through one family's eyes.
