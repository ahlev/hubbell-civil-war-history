# Dossier — Curt B. Witcher, Genealogy Center, Allen County Public Library

**1. Identity**
- Name: Curt B. Witcher
- Employer + title: Senior Manager for Special Collections, Allen County Public Library (Fort Wayne, IN); manages the Genealogy Center and the Lincoln Financial Foundation Collection. Source: https://www.acpl.lib.in.us/genealogy/power-of-story ; https://www.linkedin.com/in/curt-b-witcher-28673611/
- Background/beat: One of the most prominent figures in American genealogy librarianship — past President of the Federation of Genealogical Societies and the National Genealogical Society, and founding President of the Indiana Genealogical Society. Author of hundreds of articles for family historians, librarians, and archivists; regular contributor to the Center's *Genealogy Gems* newsletter. Source: https://ilovelibraries.org/love-my-librarian/curt-witcher/

**3. Why-them**
Witcher is both a megaphone (newsletter + national society leadership) and a thematic ally: he champions the idea that every family's story matters and that ordinary people can do serious family research. The Hubbell archive is a working proof of that thesis.

**4. Way-in (library/genealogy, "everyone has a story")**
Offer a *Genealogy Gems* contributed item or a Center program/talk: how a descendant recovered, structured, and verified 273 family letters, and a replicable method other families and small repositories could follow. Lead with the family-recovery + "what other families could do" angle; the curated-identity discipline speaks to the librarian/archivist audience.

**5. Personalization hook**
Witcher's "The Power of Story" framing — and the FamilySearch profile "Curt Witcher: Everyone Has a Story" — is precisely the spirit of recovering a five-generation family correspondence and making it usable. Source: https://www.acpl.lib.in.us/genealogy/power-of-story
