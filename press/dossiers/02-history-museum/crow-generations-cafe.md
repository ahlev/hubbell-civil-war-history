**3. Why-them**
Crow's entire platform is "you can do this" family history — making serious research approachable. The Hubbell project is a concrete, inspiring example of an amateur doing exactly that, which makes for a natural podcast guest segment or blog feature.

**4. Way-in (genealogy "what families could do" angle)**
Offer a *Generations Cafe* guest conversation or guest blog: how an amateur recovered, structured, and verified 273 family letters, with replicable, non-intimidating steps. Lead with the family-recovery arc and "what any family could do"; keep the methodology (curated alias tables, "filter never a source") accessible rather than technical.

**5. Personalization hook**
Her stated belief that "discovering your family history should be enjoyable" and her mission to show people "how to do genealogy without the frustration" is precisely the takeaway the Hubbell project offers other families.
