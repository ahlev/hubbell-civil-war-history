## 4. Way-in (history-first)
- **Lead — the discovery + the family:** Four Hubbell brothers of Champlain, NY across both theaters; 273 letters (1861–1870). Henry (34th NY) killed at Antietam — reported missing, then confirmed killed. Alexander (60th NY) wounded at Lookout Mountain, then the Atlanta Campaign. Charles and James in the 153rd NY. Mother Frances at home.
- **Cross-brother officer link:** Davis J. Rich commanded two different brothers' companies in two different regiments.
- **Method as proof point:** AI used strictly as a disciplined judge over real records — "filter, never a source."
