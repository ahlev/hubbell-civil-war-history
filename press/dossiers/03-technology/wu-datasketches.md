## 3. Why them
Wu's signature is the handcrafted, narrative visualization that treats a dataset as a canvas — her "An Interactive Visualization of Every Line in Hamilton" and "Legends" (women in film) are story-first, artful, and technically intricate. This project's nine views (especially the social network and timeline) are exactly the kind of human, culture-laden material she turns into bespoke pieces, and the structured corpus underneath gives her something real to build from.

## 4. Way-in (tech-craft lead)
- **Lead — bespoke narrative viz:** a structured corpus (486 people, 353 places, per-letter emotion/health/money fields) ready for the kind of custom, story-driven visualization she's known for — the social graph of a family at war is a Shirley Wu kind of subject.
- **Secondary — honest data:** curated identity resolution and strict fact/inference separation mean the artful layer never rests on guessed data.

## 5. Personalization hook
Her *Data Sketches* collaboration and pieces like the Hamilton line-by-line and "Legends" show her turning a single rich dataset into an emotional, interactive narrative. The pitch should offer the corpus and visuals as raw material for exactly that, peer-to-peer. (Cited: https://www.shirleywu.studio/)
