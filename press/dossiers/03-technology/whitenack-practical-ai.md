## 4. Way-in (AI-method first)
Lead with the control story. AI is used as a **judge over real retrieved records, never a source** — retrieval and judgment separated so the model classifies and cross-references but never generates a fact. 273 unstructured 1860s letters became a layered, queryable dataset with a **three-axis confidence model**, and identity is resolved by a **curated alias table, not fuzzy matching**. For a host who sells AI guardrails, the hook is: here's a small system where the guardrail is the architecture, with measurable results.
