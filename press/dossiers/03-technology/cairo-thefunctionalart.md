## 3. Why them
Cairo's defining theme is honesty in visualization — *How Charts Lie* is an entire book on it. This project's governing rule, "AI as a filter, never a source," is the same ethic applied one layer down, at the data level: provenance preserved, inferences separated from facts, confidence made explicit. He cares about both the craft of the nine visualizations and the integrity of the pipeline that feeds them, which is rare.

## 4. Way-in (tech-craft lead)
- **Lead — honest visualization craft:** nine interactive views built on a dataset designed so that fact, judgment, and computed analysis never blur — a working example of the truthful-visualization principles he teaches.
- **Secondary — pedagogy:** a clean, documented case for how to structure a primary-source archive before you ever chart it — usable in his teaching/writing on visual journalism.

## 5. Personalization hook
*How Charts Lie* and *The Truthful Art* argue that good visualization starts with honest data and explicit uncertainty. The pitch should foreground the three-axis confidence model and the strict fact/inference separation as a concrete embodiment of that argument. (Cited: https://www.albertocairo.com/)
