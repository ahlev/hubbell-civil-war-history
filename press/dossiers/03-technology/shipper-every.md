## 4. Way-in (AI-method first)
Lead with the workflow philosophy. AI is used as a **judge over real retrieved records, never a source** — retrieval and judgment kept separate so the model weighs evidence but never invents it. 273 unstructured 1860s letters became a layered, queryable dataset with a **three-axis confidence model** and **curated (not fuzzy) identity**. For AI & I, the live demo writes itself: show the queries, show the confidence scores, show what the system refuses to assert.
