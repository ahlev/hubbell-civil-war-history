## 3. Why them
Bhargava's mission is helping ordinary people make sense of data — and helping makers tell data stories that non-experts can feel. This project is a clean exemplar: a primary-source family archive turned into a legible, human dataset and nine approachable visualizations, built so that a general reader can explore a war through one family. It's both a teaching case (how to structure and present primary-source data honestly) and a story about widening who gets to do this kind of work.

## 4. Way-in (DH / data-literacy lead)
- **Lead — legibility + architecture:** making 273 letters legible at a glance through nine interactive views, on top of a three-layer data architecture (fact / editorial judgment / computed analysis) with curated person/place registries — a worked example of data storytelling done with integrity.
- **Secondary — democratizing the method:** an amateur historian, using modern AI as a disciplined filter, did what used to take a research team — directly on-theme for his data-culture work.
