## 4. Way-in (AI-method first)
Lead with the engineering discipline. The system uses AI as a **judge over real retrieved records, never a source** — a clean retrieval-vs-judgment split where the model classifies and cross-references but is structurally barred from generating a fact. 273 unstructured 1860s letters became a queryable dataset with a **three-axis confidence model**, and identity resolution is **curated, never fuzzy**. **Subject:** Built a guardrail into an archive, then measured what it refused to invent

Nathan — given your red-team instincts and the way CogRev keeps coming back to keeping models honest, I wanted to share a small build. I work in technology research in Athens, GA. I used modern AI to recover, transcribe, and structure 273 of my family's Civil War letters (1861–1870) — one writer is my great-great-grandfather and namesake.

The design choice I'd actually talk about: the model never gets to be a source. It acts only as a judge over real retrieved records — retrieval and judgment kept strictly separate — with a three-axis confidence score on every claim and curated identity matching instead of fuzzy guessing. The interesting result is everything it was structurally forbidden from fabricating.
