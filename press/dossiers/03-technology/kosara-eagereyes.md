## 3. Why them
Kosara is a working *visualization critic* — he writes about why specific chart and encoding choices succeed or fail. This project's nine visualizations (timeline, animated map, social network, health ledger, money story) are a rich, real-world case study built on a single hand-structured dataset. He's the right person to interrogate the design decisions, not just admire the output, and his research background means the data architecture underneath will interest him as much as the pixels.

## 4. Way-in (tech-craft lead)
- **Lead — viz design + criticism:** Nine interactive views over one corpus, each making a different facet (time, geography, relationships, health, money) legible. Concrete material for a "how these choices were made" discussion.
- **Secondary — data model:** the three-layer schema and curated person/place registries (486 people, 353 places) are what make the visualizations honest — the substrate behind the charts.

## 5. Personalization hook
His long-running eagereyes writing on storytelling-with-data and visualization criticism is the natural frame: offer the project as a case to dissect — what works, what he'd encode differently. (Author archive/publications: https://eagereyes.org/publications)
