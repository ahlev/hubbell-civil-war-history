## 3. Why them
Yau's entire body of work is about turning data — especially *personal*, human-scale data — into something readable and emotionally resonant ("A Day in the Life of Americans," "Years You Have Left to Live"). This project is exactly that genre at archival scale: a family's lived experience over a decade, rendered as nine interactive views. He also teaches the craft, so the methodology is as interesting to him as the output.

## 4. Way-in (tech-craft lead)
- **Lead — data-viz craft:** Nine interactive visualizations (timeline, animated map, social network, health ledger, money story) built over a single hand-structured corpus. The hook for Yau is the *pipeline*: a three-layer data architecture (fact / editorial judgment / computed analysis) that turns 273 loose letters into a queryable dataset, with 486 people and 353 places resolved into curated registries.
- **Secondary — the honest-AI discipline:** AI used as a "filter, never a source," kept out of the source-of-truth layer — a methodology angle he could teach from.
