## 3. Why them
Baio's sweet spot is the unusual personal/indie project made with real technical care — and he explicitly says he's "always looking for interesting story ideas and new, interesting projects." A descendant recovering a 160-year-old family archive with modern AI, *without* letting the model invent anything, is exactly the kind of art-meets-tech, do-it-yourself story he's championed for two decades. He's also written critically and carefully about AI training data, so the "filter, never a source" discipline will land with him rather than read as hype.

## 4. Way-in (tech-craft lead)

- **Secondary — honest AI:** AI used as a disciplined judge over real records, kept out of the source-of-truth layer — a counter-example to slop, which Baio has covered with a skeptical eye.
