## 3. Why them
Datasette is *literally* a tool for turning a pile of records into a queryable, publishable dataset — the exact thing this project did by hand to 273 letters. Willison also writes the most level-headed material on using LLMs as practical tools without overclaiming, which mirrors the "filter, never a source" rule. He is uniquely positioned to appreciate both halves: the data architecture and the disciplined AI use.

## 4. Way-in (tech-craft lead)
- **Lead — queryable-dataset craft:** 273 letters → a structured corpus with a three-layer schema (fact / editorial judgment / computed analysis), 486 people and 353 places in curated registries — conceptually a Datasette-shaped artifact. Natural angle: how a primary-source archive becomes explorable data.
- **Secondary — pragmatic AI:** AI used as a disciplined judge over real records, never the source of truth — exactly the responsible-LLM posture he advocates.

## 5. Personalization hook
His Datasette/Dogsheep work on personal-data archives, and his ongoing writing on using LLMs as honest assistants, are the direct parallel. The pitch should offer the structured data in a form he could load into Datasette, and frame the AI use in his own "tool, not oracle" terms. (Cited: https://simonwillison.net/about/)
