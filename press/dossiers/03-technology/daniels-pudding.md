## 3. Why them
The Pudding's whole form is the data-driven *visual essay* — exactly the shape this material wants to take. A family's decade of letters across four theaters of war is a culture-and-history story with a rich, structured dataset behind it: people networks, geographic movement, the lag between event and news. It's a Pudding-style essay waiting for a journalist-engineer, and Daniels is the person who decides what the collective takes on.

## 4. Way-in (tech-craft lead)
- **Lead — visual-essay-ready dataset:** 273 letters already structured into a queryable corpus (486 people, 353 places, per-letter fields), with nine working visualizations as raw material — a head start for a Pudding-style piece on, e.g., "the information delay of war" or "four brothers, four theaters."
- **Secondary — method:** curated identity resolution + "AI as filter, never a source" — a credibility story for a data-journalism shop.

## 5. Personalization hook
