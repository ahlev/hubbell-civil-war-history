## 4. Way-in (AI-method first)
Lead with the method, not the family history. The build treats AI as a **disciplined judge over real retrieved records — never a source**: a strict retrieval-vs-judgment separation where the model is allowed to weigh and classify evidence but never to generate a fact. 273 unstructured 1860s letters became a layered, queryable dataset carrying a **three-axis confidence model**, and person identity is resolved by a **curated alias table, never fuzzy matching**. It is a small, legible, reproducible example of constraining a model so it can't hallucinate its way into the archive — the discipline is the point.
