## 3. Why them
swyx defined the "AI Engineer" as the person who builds disciplined, productized systems on models rather than training them. This project is a textbook AI-engineering artifact from outside the lab: retrieval architecture, evaluation/confidence scoring, and an identity-resolution layer — the exact craft Latent Space documents.

## 4. Way-in (AI-method first)
Lead entirely on architecture. AI is used as a **judge over real retrieved records, never a source**: retrieval and judgment are separated so the model ranks, classifies, and cross-references evidence but cannot generate a fact. 273 unstructured 1860s letters were turned into a layered, queryable dataset with a **three-axis confidence model**; person identity uses a **curated alias table, not fuzzy matching** (a deliberate anti-pattern-to-the-anti-pattern). It's a clean small-scale study of designing hallucination out of a RAG-shaped system — engineering, not vibes.
