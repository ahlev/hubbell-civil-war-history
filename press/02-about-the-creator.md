## 150 words (standard bio)

Alexander Hubbell Levitt is a technology researcher based in Athens, Georgia. He is the great-great-grandson and namesake of Alexander F. Hubbell, who served four years in the 60th New York Volunteer Infantry — from Culp's Hill at Gettysburg to the Atlanta Campaign, across the same North Georgia ground where his descendant now lives.

In [2025–26], he became the fifth generation of his family to take custody of the Hubbell letters: 272 pieces of correspondence (1861–1870) between four soldier-brothers and their mother in Champlain, New York. His mother's-side ancestors saved them; a granddaughter-in-law typed every page in 1947–49; later generations introduced and digitized them. He finished the job — transcribing, structuring, and cross-referencing the collection with AI under a published rule that the model is a filter, never a source — and published the whole archive free at hubbell-civil-war-history.vercel.app.
