## 1. Government & Military Records

### 1.1 National Archives (NARA) — Civil War Records
- **URL:** https://www.archives.gov/research/military/civil-war/resources
- **Contains:** Compiled Military Service Records (CMSR), muster rolls, pay rolls, hospital records, pension applications
- **Access:** Web index + remote ordering (NATF Form 86). Digitized indexes on Ancestry/Fold3.
- **Cross-reference use:** Verify soldier service details, unit assignments, enlistment/discharge dates, injuries mentioned in letters

### 1.2 Civil War Soldiers & Sailors System (NPS)
- **URL:** https://www.nps.gov/civilwar/soldiers-and-sailors-database.htm
- **Contains:** Index of 6.3M+ soldiers (name, rank, unit), Medal of Honor recipients, POW records, 4,000+ regiment histories
- **Access:** Free web search
- **Cross-reference use:** Quick soldier identification and unit verification; starting point before deeper CMSR research

### 1.3 NPS Battle Units Search
- **URL:** https://www.nps.gov/subjects/civilwar/search-soldiers.htm
- **Contains:** Regiment battle participation, unit histories, cemetery records
- **Access:** Free web search
- **Cross-reference use:** Map a regiment to every battle it fought in — verify events described in letters

### 1.4 Civil War Pension Records
- **URLs:** Fold3 (https://www.fold3.com/publication/24/us-civil-war-widows-pensions-1861-1910), Ancestry (https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/4654/)
- **Contains:** Widow/dependent pension files, special examiner depositions, family relationship details
- **Access:** Fold3 subscription or library access; Ancestry subscription
- **Cross-reference use:** Family circumstances during/after war; witness depositions cross-reference soldier service

### 1.5 State Muster Rolls
- **Key URLs by state:**
  - Illinois: https://www.ilsos.gov/departments/archives/databases/datcivil.html
  - New York: https://museum.dmna.ny.gov/unit-history/conflict/us-civil-war-1861-1865/civil-war-muster-rolls
  - Pennsylvania: https://www.pa.gov/agencies/phmc/pa-state-archives/research-online/research-guides/civil-war-records
  - LOC guide: https://guides.loc.gov/civil-war-regimental-histories/digital-resources
- **Contains:** Muster-in/out rolls, physical descriptions, company rosters, deserter lists
- **Cross-reference use:** Unit composition, service dates, physical descriptions for identity confirmation

## 2. Genealogical Databases

### 2.1 Ancestry.com — Civil War Collections
- **URL:** https://www.ancestry.com/c/military-records/civil-war-records
- **Contains:** 18M+ names from Union/Confederate service records, POW records (1.5M+), USCT records (178K), Confederate pardon applications
- **Access:** Subscription
- **Cross-reference use:** Comprehensive indexed military service records with high-quality document scans

### 2.2 FamilySearch — Civil War Resources
- **URL:** https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Beginning_United_States_Civil_War_Research
- **Contains:** CMSR (via Fold3), Registers of Enlistments 1798–1914, Naval Enlistments, Union Pension Records
- **Access:** Free (some records require library card)
- **Cross-reference use:** Free alternative to commercial services; pension and naval records

### 2.3 Fold3 — Military Records
- **URL:** https://www.fold3.com/collection/us-civil-war
- **Contains:** 101M+ records across 184 publications; Brady photographs; Southern Claims Commission; service records, pensions, photos
- **Access:** Subscription or free via many public libraries
- **Cross-reference use:** Brady photographs for soldier identification; cross-links between service records, pensions, photos

### 2.4 FindAGrave
- **URL:** https://www.findagrave.com/virtual-cemetery/112466
- **Contains:** Burial locations, gravestone photos, family relationships, user-contributed history
- **Access:** Free
- **Cross-reference use:** Locate burial sites, verify death dates, discover family connections

### 2.5 SUVCW Graves Registration Database
- **URL:** https://www.suvcwdb.org/
- **Contains:** 4M+ names with burial info, birth/death dates, unit/rank, enlistment/discharge dates, cemetery details
- **Access:** Free
- **Cross-reference use:** Verify death dates, locate comrades' graves for casualty context in letters

## 3. Battle & Campaign Data

### 3.1 American Battlefield Trust
- **URL:** https://www.battlefields.org/learn/civil-war
- **Contains:** 10,000+ battles/engagements, casualty info, 100+ battle maps, animated battle progression maps
- **Access:** Free web + mobile app
- **Cross-reference use:** Verify battle dates/locations/casualties; animated maps for understanding troop movements in letters

### 3.2 American Civil War Battle Data (CSV)
- **URL:** https://acw-battle-data.readthedocs.io/en/latest/data.html
- **Contains:** 384 principal battles from CWSAC; dates, locations, campaigns, theaters, military significance
- **Format:** Downloadable CSV
- **Cross-reference use:** PROGRAMMATIC cross-referencing of letter dates against battle timelines. Key structured dataset for our system.

### 3.3 Esri Civil War StoryMap
- **URL:** https://storymaps.esri.com/stories/civilwar/
- **Contains:** Interactive map of major battles, searchable by name, sortable by date
- **Cross-reference use:** Geographic visualization of campaign progression mentioned in letters

## 4. Newspaper Archives

### 4.1 Chronicling America (Library of Congress)
- **URL:** https://chroniclingamerica.loc.gov/
- **Contains:** 3,000+ newspaper titles (1789–1963), full-text searchable
- **Access:** Free
- **Cross-reference use:** Contemporary battle accounts, casualty lists, home front news. Local papers often mention hometown soldiers by name.

### 4.2 State-Specific Newspaper Archives
- Pennsylvania: https://libraries.psu.edu/about/collections/digital-newspapers/pennsylvania-civil-war-era-newspaper-collection
- Virginia: https://lva-virginia.libguides.com/civil-war/newspapers
- Alabama: https://digital.archives.alabama.gov/digital/collection/cwnp
- Maryland: https://lib.guides.umd.edu/c.php?g=326774&p=2197451

## 5. Letters & Diaries Collections (for Comparative Analysis)

### 5.1 Valley of the Shadow
- **URL:** https://valley.newamericanhistory.org/
- **Contains:** 20+ searchable databases; thousands of letters/diaries; census, government records; Augusta Co. VA & Franklin Co. PA (1855–1870)
- **Access:** Free, XML-coded and fully searchable
- **Cross-reference use:** Compare Northern/Southern perspectives; find references to same battles/units/dates

### 5.2 University Digital Collections

## 6. Regimental Histories

### 6.1 Library of Congress — Regimental Histories Guide
- **URL:** https://guides.loc.gov/civil-war-regimental-histories
- **Contains:** Comprehensive guide to finding unit histories, links to digital/print resources, organized by state
- **Cross-reference use:** Locate detailed published histories for regiments mentioned in letters

### 6.2 Civil War Archive — Regimental Index
- **URL:** https://www.civilwararchive.com/regim.htm
- **Contains:** Listing of all regiments by state/number
- **Cross-reference use:** Quick regiment identification and navigation

### 6.3 American Civil War Research Database
- **URL:** https://www.civilwardata.com/
- **Contains:** 4M+ soldiers in a searchable database
- **Cross-reference use:** Comprehensive soldier lookup with extensive indexing

## 8. Recommended Cross-Reference Workflow

1. **Identify soldiers** → CWSS → Ancestry/FamilySearch for full CMSR
2. **Verify unit & battles** → ABT + ACW Battle Data CSV + CWSS Regiment Histories
3. **Find newspaper accounts** → Chronicling America + state archives
4. **Compare with other letters** → Valley of the Shadow + university collections
5. **Verify family/post-war** → Pension records + FindAGrave + SUVCW
6. **Build timeline** → ACW Battle Data CSV + letter dates → programmatic cross-reference
