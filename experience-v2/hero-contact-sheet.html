Layer 1 — Sky

deepest backdrop · cold winter dawn · full-frame, opaque is fine

Mostly sits behind the valley + tent, so the upper sky and horizon glow are what read. All three hold the muted grey-blue + amber-horizon palette.

Layer 2 — Valley encampment

seen through the tent mouth · top fades to fog

The recon view: rows of tents in fog, campfires, winding road, receding hills, tiny anonymous silhouettes. This is what shows through the tent opening as you parallax.

Layer 3 — Tent mouth (POV frame)

frame-within-frame · center reads through to the valley

Sells the point of view. In the wired hero a soft CSS mask opens the center so the valley shows through. tent-v1 has the cleanest wide opening to key out; tent-v2 is the most cinematic but paints its own camp + silhouette through the gap (works better as a near-complete scene than as a see-through frame).

Layer 4 — Field writing kit (foreground)

the hero object · lantern-lit · top fades out

The reaching-home detail: crate/blanket desk, unfinished letter (illegible by design), tin cup, candle, lantern (lower-right per the light rule), forage cap, rifle butt. v1 reads warmest with a tan kepi; v2/v3 use the blue Union forage cap — more accurate for the Hubbells' NY regiments.

Motion — foreground ambient loop

Seedance 2.0 · 5s · silent · locked camera

Animates only the foreground: lantern + candle flicker, a faint smoke wisp, breath of steam off the cup. In the wired hero this can swap in for the field layer so it still parallaxes. Two variants, matching the two field looks.
