Scenes a NY infantryman's war

Net-new compositions beyond the tent vantage — the lived experience across seasons, from the mundane to the harrowing. Intensity (skirmish, hospital) is carried by smoke, shadow and posture; wounded are anonymous and restrained, never graphic — the same painterly register that protects the "AI-enabled historian" brand. Two versions each.

Smoky skirmish. Union line takes cover behind a stone wall under fire; wounded suggested, not shown.
The advance. A battle line crosses open ground through powder smoke — the dread of the exposed advance.
The march. A column moving out down a dusty road — the weary endlessness between battles.
Picket duty. A lone sentry on the night watch at a winter treeline — solitude and cold.
Letter by the fire. Soldiers around a campfire, one reading a letter from home — the heart of the archive.
Winter quarters. Log huts, stacked muskets, frozen company street — the long boredom of winter camp.
Field hospital. A surgeon's tent at dusk, stretcher-bearers, covered forms — the cost, told with restraint.

Transition the year turning

Spring → war-marked winter, one valley, the in-betweens painted by the model (Seedance 2.0; start-frame = spring valley, end-frame = winter valley).

This is the Tier-4 technique from the plan: let the model paint the seasonal in-betweens, drop the clip into a layer's data-layer-video slot, and scrub it by scroll — the cheapest path to a cinematic season morph.

Full strategy — scroll-scrub crossfade, displacement-dissolve shaders, dawn→afternoon light shifts, the spring-field→battlefield two-pass — is in tasks/experience-v2/SEASON-TRANSITIONS.md.

Picks & direction welcome: which season variants to keep, which scenes to use where, and whether to wire the scroll-scrub year-turn into the hero. Full-res 4K masters sit beside every web derivative in assets/seasons/ and assets/scenes/.
